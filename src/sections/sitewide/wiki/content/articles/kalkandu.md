Kalkandu is crystallised sugar — large, translucent diamonds that dissolve slowly and
sweeten gently. South Indian kitchens drop it into [kheer](/wiki/cooking-techniques/milk-reduction/) and paal
payasam, where its slow melt suits the long simmer, and it appears in prasadam mixtures and
herbal conserves where a cooling, mild sweet is wanted.

It reads faintly milky-sweet next to [jaggery's](/wiki/ingredients/jaggery/) molasses depth, which
is why the two lead different kinds of sweets rather than competing.

No vegetable travelled further than the potato — Andean in origin, Indian by adoption,
and now the substance of aloo-dum and sambar's companion piece, of vada-pav and
dum-biryani. The home kitchen stopped asking where it came from long ago.

The temple canon did not: the pre-Columbian boundaries kept by Puri's Rosaghara still
exclude it, with the [cashew](/wiki/ingredients/cashew/) and the [chili](/wiki/ingredients/green-chili/), from sanctum
offerings. Recipes on this site note the distinction where it matters — the home pot and
the sanctum pot follow different histories.

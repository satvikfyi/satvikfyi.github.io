## In Āyurveda

Dhānyaka is the answer to the question every pitta summer asks. Cooling where its cousin cumin is
merely calm, it is one of the few spices the tradition hands freely to inflamed stomachs and feverish
bodies, the whole plant, root to leaf, is food.

## In Āyurveda

Jīraka shares its root with jāraṇa, digestion. The texts prize it for doing what few pungents
manage: waking a sleepy gut while staying gentle enough for inflamed pitta stomachs. That is why it,
not something hotter, is the seed nearly every dal begins with.

Chana dal is split desi chickpea — smaller, sweeter and nuttier than most dals, holding
its shape when that is wanted (kosambari salads, temple sundals) and grinding to a paste
when it is not (the roasted spice pastes of Odia cooking, ambode vadas).

In [panch-phoron](/wiki/ingredients/panch-phoron/) country it thickens; in Udupi's kitchens it
balances [toor dal](/wiki/ingredients/toor-dal/) in the saaru bowl. Roasted dry it becomes
putani, the snack that grinds into powders.

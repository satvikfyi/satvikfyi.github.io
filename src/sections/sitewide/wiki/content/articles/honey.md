## In Āyurveda

Madhu is the exception among sweets: the texts call it drying and scraping, the friend of kapha and
the enemy of congestion. And it carries the tradition's most famous food rule, never heated; a
prohibition repeated for a thousand years and easy to honour: the tea cools first, then the honey.

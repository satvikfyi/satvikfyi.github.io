In 1946 an Indian swami in Los Angeles published his life story, and the West's image of yoga
changed overnight. Yogananda's autobiography, tigers, deathless Himalayan babas, his guru
Sri Yukteswar, and the science of Kriya Yoga; reads like scripture dressed as an adventure, which
is roughly its own claim.

## Why it endures

The chapters on his mother, his guru's discipline, and the quiet description of yoga as "a method
to restrain the natural turbulence of thoughts" carry real teaching. The book founded the
Self-Realization Fellowship and set the template for yoga literature in English.

## How to read it now

Take the miracles as the tradition's own genre, hagiography with a smile, and the seeking as
the report. This site's [meditation pages](/mind/meditation/) teach the same stillness Yogananda
described, minus the tigers.

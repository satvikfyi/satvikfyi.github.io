On Ekadashi and the Navratri fasts the kitchen changes grammar. Plain salt is set
aside for [rock salt](/wiki/ingredients/rock-salt/) — sendha namak, the fasting salt. Grains
and pulses are avoided, so the table turns to what is allowed:
[sabudana](/wiki/ingredients/sabudana/), kuttu (buckwheat), singhara (water chestnut), rajgira,
potatoes, curd and fruits.

The vrat recipes here follow those rules strictly; the satvikTags carry `vrat` when a dish
qualifies. The logic is as much digestive as devotional in the tradition — a lighter,
rougher, cleansing day — and the cooked results are anything but austere.

Kheer is patience made edible: milk simmered low and long, the cream scraped back
into the pot each time it rises, until the milk colour turns ivory and the volume falls by
half or more. Rice (or vermicelli, or dal) cooks in the milk itself — akkara vadisal takes
rice and moong dal in milk alone, without a drop of water.

[Kalkandu](/wiki/ingredients/kalkandu/) or jaggery sweetens late, so the milk does not
break; cardamom and camphor finish. The other path is Palani's — panchamirtham, the
waterless preserve where sugar's own osmosis does the keeping instead of the fire's. Both
are temple logic: sweetness that holds.

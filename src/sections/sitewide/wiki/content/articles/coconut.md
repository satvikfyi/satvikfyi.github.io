A fresh coconut, cracked and scraped, is a different grocery from everything it becomes
by drying or pressing: copra, the dried meat, coconut milk, and coconut oil are
four distinct items in the canonical list, and recipes are right to be particular.

Fresh grated coconut finishes the South temple plate — folded into kosambari, ground into
chutneys with [curry leaves](/wiki/ingredients/curry-leaves/) and dal, stirred through vegetable
poriyals, crowning ghee-rich akkara vadisal. In Kerala's
kitchens it is nearly a cooking medium itself. The scraping is the labour; the reward is the
aroma, which is why "grated" moved into the canonical name — it is the form the recipes
mean.

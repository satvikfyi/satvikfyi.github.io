Where Śiva dissolves, Viṣṇu maintains; the pair forming, with Brahmā, the tradition's best-known
triad of creator, preserver and dissolver. Viṣṇu is pictured recumbent on the serpent Ananta, the
lotus of the next universe rising from his navel: preservation as patient, cosmic sleep.

## The descents

The doctrine of *avatāra* holds that Viṣṇu descends when [dharma](/wiki/concepts/dharma/) declines,
 classically ten times, from the fish and tortoise of the old flood myths to Rāma and Kṛṣṇa. It
is as Kṛṣṇa, charioteer and teacher, that he speaks the [Bhagavad Gītā](/wiki/scriptures/bhagavad-gita/).

## In the living tradition

Vaiṣṇava worship, Rāma and Kṛṣṇa lineages above all; is among the largest devotional streams in
India, and the mahā-mantra of the bhakti traditions calls his names (it lives in the
[mantra collection](/mind/mantras/)).

If the [Yoga Sūtras](/wiki/scriptures/yoga-sutras/) are the theory, *Light on Yoga* is the
twentieth century's great answer in practice: some two hundred postures, each photographed,
graded by difficulty, and prescribed by ailment, prefaced by an introduction to yoga philosophy
that Iyengar wrote at his editor's insistence and that became a classic on its own.

## What it changed

The book, and Iyengar's teaching; brought precision, props and therapeutic application to a
practice that had been transmitted loosely, and carried it to the West. Nearly every asana
reference on this site's [yoga pages](/body/yoga/) stands on its shoulders.

## Honest cautions

Iyengar's own practice was extreme; the book's later poses demand decades. Its listings of
contraindications were pioneering for 1966 and are thinner than today's standards, this site's
pose entries carry fuller ones.

Black salt is kiln-fired rock salt, and its colour runs plum-brown rather than true black;
the grind smells faintly of sulphur, which is exactly the point. A pinch transforms raita,
chaat, jaljeera and spiced buttermilk with an umami edge no other salt gives.

In this kitchen the family treats black salt and [rock salt](/wiki/ingredients/rock-salt/) as
cousins — sometimes interchangeable, both chosen for flavour rather than plain saltiness —
while plain [salt](/wiki/ingredients/salt/) stays a separate grocery for the everyday pot.

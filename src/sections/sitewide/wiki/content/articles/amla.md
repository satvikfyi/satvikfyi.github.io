## In Āyurveda

Āmalakī is the fruit the classical rasāyana chapters cannot stop praising: cooling yet
digestion-kindling, tridoshic, sour-sweet-astringent all at once. The raw fruit makes the mouth
pucker; tradition says that astringency is exactly where its strength lives.

The green chili is a colonial-era arrival that India took to with total commitment —
slit into dals, fried whole into temperings, blistered into chutneys. Heat in the modern
kitchen means capsicum's fruit.

The archaic temple kitchens drew the line: at Puri the sanctum's heat still comes from
black pepper, long pepper and ginger — the pre-Columbian trilogy — and the chili stays
outside with the [potato](/wiki/ingredients/potato/). Both kitchens are honoured in this
collection; the recipes say which they follow.

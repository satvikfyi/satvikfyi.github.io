## In Āyurveda

Caraka calls ginger viśvabheṣaja, "the universal medicine", and no Indian kitchen has ever been
without it. Fresh ginger cools into its pungency; dried ginger burns hotter, the same rhizome, two
temperaments, chosen by the dosha of the day.

Satvik.fyi began with a simple irritation: the practical wisdom of the Indian traditions, how to eat,
how to breathe, how to work, how to sit still; is scattered across a thousand sites, most of them
selling something, few of them citing anything.

We wanted the site we could not find:

- **Source-first.** Classical claims carry their citations, the texts and verses they come from; 
  and modern interpretation is labelled as modern.
- **Non-dogmatic.** The traditions disagree with each other, gloriously; we present them as they are,
  not as one flattened instruction manual.
- **Private.** No accounts, no trackers, no cookies. Your dosha quiz result and meal plan live in
  your browser's local storage and nowhere else.
- **Free, always.** This is a non-profit effort. There is nothing to buy and nothing will be
  paywalled.

## What is already here

The [Body pillar](/body/) carries the [satvik recipe collection](/body/meals/) with a
[weekly planner](/body/meals/planner/), the [asana database](/body/yoga/), and the
[herbs & ingredients reference](/body/ayurveda/). The [Mind pillar](/mind/) holds
[prāṇāyāma](/mind/pranayama/) with honest safety notes, [meditation practices](/mind/meditation/),
and the [mantra collection](/mind/mantras/). The [Soul pillar](/soul/) maps the classical paths, 
[karma](/soul/action/), [gyan](/soul/knowledge/), [bhakti](/soul/devotion/), the
[eight limbs](/soul/meditation/), and a careful [introduction to tantra](/soul/tantra/).

A [dosha quiz](/quiz/) ties the practical side together: answer twelve quiet questions and the
recipes, asanas, herbs and breathwork all filter themselves to your constitution.

## What is coming

A wiki of scriptures, deities and concepts; sitewide search; and this blog, which will carry essays
and working notes as the site grows. If that sounds worth following, the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) and
the newsletter (bottom of any page) are the ways.

Welcome, and if something here is wrong, tell us; that is what the comments are for.

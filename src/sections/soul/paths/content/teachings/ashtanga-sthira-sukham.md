Two qualities, held together: sthira (steadiness, strength, alertness) and sukha (ease, comfort,
softness). Neither alone is a posture, a pose that is steady but rigid is sculpture; comfortable but
collapsing is a couch. Patañjali wants both at once, and the next sūtra names the method:
"loosening of effort and meditation upon the boundless."

For a sūtra about sitting for meditation, it has had a long afterlife in the world's yoga studios; 
every alignment cue, prop and modification is an argument between sthira and sukha. This site's whole
asana collection quietly obeys these three words.

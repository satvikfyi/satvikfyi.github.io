A definition so compact that every commentator has had to unfold it. "Skill" (kauśala) is not mere
proficiency, the commentaries gloss it as the wisdom that acts at the right time, in the right
measure, without the self jamming the machinery. Attention itself, fully given, is most of the skill.

Read with 2.47 (the action, not the fruit) the verse completes the picture: what detachment removes,
skill supplies. The karma yogi is not the one who cares least about the work, but the one whose
caring has stopped being about themselves, which is, incidentally, when the work gets good.

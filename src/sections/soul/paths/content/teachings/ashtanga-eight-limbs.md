One sūtra, eight words, the whole curriculum. The order is the teaching: ethics (yama, niyama) before
body (āsana), body before breath, breath before senses, senses before attention, and only then
dhāraṇā, dhyāna, samādhi, the inner three that no effort reaches directly.

Modern yoga classes live mostly on limbs three and four; this site's body pillar feeds the first four,
the mind pillar the fifth and sixth, and this page names them all; because the tradition is explicit
that the limbs support one another like a building's storeys, not like a menu.

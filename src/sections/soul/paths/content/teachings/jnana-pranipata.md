The most counter-cultural verse in the knowledge chapter: the way to truth begins not with argument
but with praṇipāta, bowing down. The sequence matters. Humility opens the ear; inquiry
(paripraśna, real questioning, not debating) sharpens it; service (sevā) pays for it.

"Tattva-darśinaḥ", seers of truth, not merely quoters of it; is the qualification the verse demands
of the teacher. The gyan path is thus not solitary; the Upaniṣads themselves are largely
conversations, student to seer, and this verse is their protocol.

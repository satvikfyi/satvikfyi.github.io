Where some schools read the world as māyā, illusion to be seen through; the tantric traditions read
it as śakti: the goddess's own energy, every particle of it. The refrain above, chanted through the
Devī Māhātmya to this day, states it exactly: she abides in *all* beings *as* power. Nothing is
outside; nothing is merely junk to be renounced.

This site treats tantra as the introductor's does, as a family of traditions with a distinctive,
honourable metaphysics. Its famous ritual elaborations belong to lineages that transmit them in
person; the idea, however, belongs to everyone: the world as sacred energy, and power as the face of
the divine.

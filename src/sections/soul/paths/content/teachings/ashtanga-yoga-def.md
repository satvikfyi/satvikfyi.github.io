Patañjali defines yoga not as posture, breath or even meditation, but as the condition when the
mind's turning (vṛtti) stills. The next sūtra adds the reason it matters: "then the seer rests in its
own nature." Everything else in the 195 sūtras, including the eight limbs; is scaffolding for this
one definition.

This site's whole meditation page is downstream of this sentence; the soul-pillar's ashtanga pages map
the scaffolding. Four words, and a psychology two millennia old that modern attention research keeps
arriving beside.

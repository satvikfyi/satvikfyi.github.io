Seventeen chapters of philosophy, practice and duty, and the last substantive instruction is not
another discipline but a surrender. The bhakti schools have always read this as the Gītā's
destination, prapatti, śaraṇāgati, the handing-over of the whole burden including the burden of
being one's own saviour.

"Mā śucaḥ", do not grieve; ends the verse and nearly ends the Gītā; Arjuna's problem, from chapter
1, had been exactly that. The verse is not a licence for passivity in the bhakti reading, but the
reversal of the doer: effort continues, ownership ends.

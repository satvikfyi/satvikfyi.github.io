Five syllables, and an entire civilization of repetition. The Tamil saints sang it through streets and
kingdoms; modern kirtan carried it to every yoga studio on earth, the video linked here is one
American singer's version that tens of millions have sat with. The name means "the auspicious", and
that is the whole theology.

Included here honestly labelled: this is India's Buddhist tradition's mantra, carried out of India and
kept alive most vividly in the Himalayas. The six syllables are studied a lifetime, the Dalai Lama's
own summary of them is disarmingly simple: the path of the jewel (compassion's method) and the lotus
(wisdom), held together in one phrase.

summary: The simplest closing in the tradition, peace said three times, for the three places it must hold.
text: ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः
transliteration: "oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ"
meaning: "Oṁ, peace, peace, peace. The commentaries hear the threefold repetition as peace in the heavens (weather, fate), peace among living beings (the world), and peace in oneself."
meter: Free closing invocation
usage: "Spoken or sung at the end of mantras, study, classes, and gatherings of every kind; the full stop of the tradition. Also used alone, any moment peace is called for, including public ones."

---

Three words, and an entire theory of where trouble comes from; above, around, and inside. The chant
does not distinguish: it asks for calm in all three at once, and then stops, which is itself the
lesson.

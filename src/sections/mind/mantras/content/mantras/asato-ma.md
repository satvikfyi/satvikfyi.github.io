name: "Asato Mā: the Upaniṣadic Prayer"

tradition: Vedic (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad)

summary: Three requests and three shāntis, lead me from the unreal to the real, darkness to light, death to deathlessness.
text: असतो मा सद्गमय तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः
transliteration: "asato mā sad-gamaya tamaso mā jyotir-gamaya mṛtyor mā amṛtaṁ gamaya oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ"
meaning: "From the unreal, lead me to the real; from darkness, lead me to light; from death, lead me to deathlessness. Oṁ: peace, peace, peace."
meter: Prose prayer (brāhmī gīti) closing with the threefold śānti
usage: "Chanted at the close of study and satsaṅga across every tradition; the three śāntis are traditionally explained as peace with the elements, the world, and oneself. A communal chant, sung slowly, together."

---

A prayer that asks for direction rather than things: not "give me truth" but "lead me toward it". The
threefold śānti at the end grants peace in advance of arrival, the tradition's way of saying the
walking itself is the gift.

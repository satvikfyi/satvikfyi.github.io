---
name: Left-Nostril Cooling Breath
slug: chandra-bhedana
sanskritName: Candra Bhedana Prāṇāyāma
category: cooling
level: intermediate
summary: The moon to Sūrya Bhedana's sun, in through the left nostril, out through the right; drawing coolness into an overheated system.
benefits:
  - Cools the body and settles heat-driven irritation
  - Invites sleep when practised before bed
  - Balances the activated right side after heating practices or hot days
  - A quiet aid for pitta conditions of heat and hurry
contraindications:
  - Avoid when already chilled, in winter sluggishness, or kapha excess
  - Depression or heavy low moods, prefer balancing practices instead
  - Never force the retention
steps:
  - Sit tall; close the right nostril with the thumb.
  - Inhale slowly and fully through the left nostril.
  - Close both nostrils; hold briefly and comfortably.
  - Release the right nostril and exhale slowly through it.
  - Begin with 6–8 rounds, ending gently.
rhythm: Left-nostril inhale; brief hold; right-nostril exhale.
durationGuidance: 6–8 rounds in the evening, in heat, or after heating practices.
associatedDoshas:
  - pitta
sources:
  - title: Light on Pranayama
    author: B. K. S. Iyengar
    reference: the lunar breath as the complement of the solar
  - title: Asana Pranayama Mudra Bandha
    author: Swami Satyananda Saraswati
---

Candra, the moon; rules the iḍā nāḍī, the left, the cool channel. The older texts name the solar
breath explicitly and leave the lunar implied as its mirror; the modern lineage of Iyengar and
Satyananda teaches the pair together, and no Indian needs to be told what moonlight does to heat.

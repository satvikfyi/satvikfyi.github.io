---
name: Ocean Breath
slug: ujjayi
sanskritName: Ujjāyī Prāṇāyāma
category: calming
level: beginner
summary: The soft "ocean sound" breath, a gentle constriction at the throat that turns each breath into a slow wave, warming and steadying.
benefits:
  - Slows and lengthens the breath without force
  - Warms the body gently; steadies attention with its sound
  - Soothes the nervous system, traditional support for vāta restlessness
  - The classic breath of āsana flow practice
contraindications:
  - Keep the throat soft, any strain, cough or loud hissing means the constriction is too tight
  - Avoid in acute throat infection or fever
  - Not while overheated on hot days, its warmth accumulates
steps:
  - Sit or stand tall; breathe naturally through the nose.
  - Slightly constrict the back of the throat, as if fogging a mirror, but with the mouth closed.
  - "Inhale, a soft sibilant sound from the throat, like a distant wave."
  - "Exhale, the same sound, a shade longer; the ocean receding."
  - Let the sound be quiet enough that only you hear it; 8–12 slow rounds.
rhythm: "1:0:2 as comfort grows (exhale about twice the inhale)"
durationGuidance: A few minutes inside āsana practice, or 8–12 rounds seated on its own.
associatedDoshas:
  - vata
  - pitta
sources:
  - title: Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā
    author: Svātmārāma
    reference: chapter 2, verses 51–53; the victorious breath removing decay and death
  - title: Light on Pranayama
    author: B. K. S. Iyengar
---

Ujjāyī means "victorious", the breath that conquers the wandering mind by giving the ear something
steady to follow. The Pradīpikā credits it grandly; modern practice values it more humbly and more
often: it is the soundtrack of nearly every vinyāsa class in the world.

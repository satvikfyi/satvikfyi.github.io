---
name: Right-Nostril Activating Breath
slug: surya-bhedana
sanskritName: Sūrya Bhedana Prāṇāyāma
category: energizing
level: intermediate
summary: Breathe in through the right nostril, out through the left; waking the solar channel for cold mornings and low fire.
benefits:
  - Kindles digestive fire and body heat (the "solar" nāḍī)
  - Dispels kapha coldness, damp and low energy
  - Traditionally said to cleanse the sinuses and dispel intestinal worms (the texts' claim, offered as tradition)
  - Useful before effort, study, cold work, early practice
contraindications:
  - Avoid with fever, inflammation or any heat condition; it adds heat
  - Pitta constitutions should use rarely and briefly
  - High blood pressure, avoid without guidance
  - Never force the retention; the hold is optional and small
steps:
  - Sit tall; close the left nostril with the ring finger.
  - Inhale slowly and fully through the right nostril.
  - Close both nostrils and hold briefly, comfortably.
  - Release the left nostril and exhale slowly through it.
  - That is one round, begin with 6–8 rounds.
rhythm: Right-nostril inhale; brief hold; left-nostril exhale.
durationGuidance: 6–8 rounds in the morning or before cold-weather effort.
associatedDoshas:
  - kapha
  - vata
sources:
  - title: Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā
    author: Svātmārāma
    reference: chapter 2, verse 50; the activating breath that destroys intestinal worms and sinuses' ills (traditional claims)
  - title: Asana Pranayama Mudra Bandha
    author: Swami Satyananda Saraswati
---

Sūrya, the sun; is the right side in the subtle cartography of haṭha yoga, the piṅgalā nāḍī of heat
and effort. The texts' medical claims (worms among them) belong to their era; the heat, however, is
directly felt by anyone who tries it on a cold morning.

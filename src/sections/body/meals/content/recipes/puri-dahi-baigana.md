ingredients:
  - item: "eggplant"
    quantity: "1 medium"
    note: "sliced into 1/2-inch thick rounds"
  - item: "curd (yogurt)"
    quantity: "1.5 cups"
    note: "fresh, whisked smooth with 1/4 cup cold water"
  - item: "turmeric powder"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "mustard oil"
    quantity: "1 tbsp"
  - item: "mustard seeds"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "panch phoron"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
    note: "equal parts cumin, fennel, fenugreek, mustard, kalonji"
  - item: "dry red chili"
    quantity: 1
    note: "broken in half"
  - item: "hing (asafoetida)"
    quantity: "1/4 tsp"
  - item: "curry leaves"
    quantity: "1 sprig"
  - item: "cumin powder"
    note: "roasted"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1 tsp"
    note: "divided"

steps:
  - "Dust the sliced eggplant rounds with the turmeric powder and 1/2 teaspoon of salt."
  - "Heat the mustard oil in a wide skillet over medium flame."
  - "Arrange the eggplant rounds in the skillet and pan-fry for 4 to 5 minutes per side until deeply browned and tender to the core; remove to a plate."
  - "In a wide, shallow bowl, whisk the yogurt with 1/4 cup cold water, remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt, and the roasted cumin powder."
  - "Gently arrange the warm pan-fried eggplant rounds in the yogurt, spooning curd over the top to coat them."
  - "Heat 1 teaspoon of mustard oil in a small tempering ladle; add the mustard seeds and panch phoron until they crackle."
  - "Add the broken dried red chili, curry leaves, and hing; sauté for 10 seconds."
  - "Pour the hot tempering directly over the eggplant and yogurt."
  - "Rest for 10 minutes at room temperature before serving alongside rice or khichari."
---

Dahi Baigana is a refreshing component of the Jagannath Puri temple feast. Pan-frying the eggplant rounds in pungent mustard oil caramelizes the flesh, which softens further when submerged in whisked, seasoned cultured curd.

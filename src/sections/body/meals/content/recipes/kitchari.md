steps:
  - Rinse the rice and dal together until the water runs clear; drain.
  - Heat the ghee in a heavy pot and pop the mustard seeds, then add cumin, ginger, curry leaves, turmeric and asafoetida.
  - Add the rice and dal and stir to coat in the spiced ghee for a minute.
  - Pour in the water and salt, bring to a boil, then simmer partially covered 25–30 minutes until everything is very soft and porridge-like.
  - Rest 5 minutes, stir well, and serve garnished with coriander and an extra spoon of ghee.

Kitchari sits at the heart of ayurvedic cookery: rice and mung together make a complete, easily
absorbed protein that is gentle enough for the sick and satisfying enough for everyone else. During
panchakarma, the classical cleansing regimen; patients eat nothing but kitchari for days so that
agni can rest while the body still receives nourishment. Soft, warm and mildly spiced, it is the
one dish traditionally said to balance all three doshas at once.

steps:
  - "In a heavy pot, combine the 7 diced vegetables, 2 cups of water, turmeric powder, salt, and the tamarind liquid."
  - "Boil uncovered over medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes until the vegetables are cooked through."
  - "In a dry skillet, roast 1 tablespoon of chana dal, coriander seeds, and dry red chilies over low flame until golden."
  - "Grind the roasted spices with the fresh grated coconut and 1/4 cup water into a smooth paste."
  - "Add the cooked chana dal and the ground roasted coconut-spice paste to the pot of tender vegetables."
  - "Simmer over medium-low heat for 6 minutes, stirring gently, until the stew thickens into a rich, cohesive consistency."
  - "Heat the ghee in a small pan; add mustard seeds and let them splutter."
  - "Add the curry leaves and hing; sauté for 10 seconds."
  - "Pour the hot ghee tempering directly into the bubbling kootu."
  - "Cover and rest for 10 minutes before serving alongside festive rice preparations."

Ezhu Kari Kootu ('seven-vegetable stew') is prepared during the sacred winter month of Margazhi and the festival of Thiruvathirai at Srirangam. Rooted in seasonal eating, it brings together seven native roots, gourds, and pods, binding them with roasted pulse starches and coconut.

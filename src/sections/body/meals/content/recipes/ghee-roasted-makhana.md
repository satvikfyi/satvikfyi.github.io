  - item: ghee

  - item: black pepper
    quantity: ¼ tsp
    note: coarsely ground
  - item: rock salt
    quantity: ¼ tsp
steps:
  - Warm the ghee in a wide pan over medium-low heat.
  - Add the makhana and roast, stirring constantly, for 6–8 minutes until they are crisp and just beginning to gold.
  - Sprinkle over the pepper and rock salt; toss for another 30 seconds off the heat.
  - Cool completely, they crisp further as they cool, and store in an airtight jar for 2–3 days.

Makhana are the popped seeds of the lotus, the very plant the tradition holds up as the emblem of
purity, growing clean out of the mud. Unsurprisingly they became the food of choice for vrats and
sādhanā seasons: light enough not to dull the mind, roasting in minutes, and keeping their snap for
days. Roast them low and slow; scorched makhana stay stubbornly chewy.

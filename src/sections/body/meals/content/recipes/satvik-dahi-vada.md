steps:
  - "In a wide bowl, combine the 2 cups of warm water with 1/2 teaspoon regular salt and 1/4 teaspoon hing."
  - "Drop the freshly deep-fried, warm urad dal vadas into the warm hing water; soak for 15 minutes until softened and pale."
  - "Gently press each vada between the palms of your hands to squeeze out excess water without breaking the dumpling."
  - "In a separate bowl, whisk together the fresh yogurt, fine sugar, black salt, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon regular salt until smooth."
  - "Arrange the pressed vadas in a single layer in a wide, flat serving dish."
  - "Pour the sweetened whisked yogurt generously over the vadas, submerging them completely."
  - "Dust the tops evenly with the roasted cumin powder."
  - "Drizzle ribbons of sweet tamarind chutney across the yogurt and garnish with fresh cilantro."
  - "Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving cool."

Dahi Vada holds an exalted place across both North and South Indian temple banquets. Soaking the warm pulse dumplings in salted hing water purges surface frying fats and tenderizes the interior. When coated in chilled, sweetened yogurt, the dish provides a soothing counterpoint to fiery festival courses.

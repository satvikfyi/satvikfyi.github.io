On Sri Rama Navami throughout South India, Kadale Bele Kosambari is traditionally distributed alongside Panakam and Majjige. The pairing of toothsome soaked Bengal gram with sweet grated carrots and fresh coconut meat provides an earthy texture that contrasts with warm, cooked dishes on the plantain leaf.

steps:
  - Soak the cashews and poppy seeds in warm water for 15 minutes.
  - Blend the coconut, cashews, poppy seeds, ginger and chili with a little water into a smooth paste.
  - Heat the ghee and sizzle the whole spices for 30 seconds.
  - Add the vegetables and sauté 3–4 minutes, then pour in the water, cover, and cook until just tender, about 8 minutes.
  - Stir in the coconut paste and salt; simmer gently 5–6 minutes until the gravy thickens and no longer tastes raw.
  - Finish with garam masala and serve with pulao or phulka.

Korma in its satvik form skips onion, garlic and even tomato entirely: the body of the gravy comes
from fresh coconut and nuts ground to silk. That makes it cooling and nourishing, a festival dish
for autumn and winter that soothes pitta while giving vāta the unctuousness it craves. Kapha does
best with a modest helping.

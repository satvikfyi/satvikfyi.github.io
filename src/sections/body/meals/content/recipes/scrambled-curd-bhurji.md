ingredients:
  - item: "chhena (fresh curd cheese)"
    quantity: "2 cups"
    note: "fresh from 2 liters milk, drained 5 minutes, moist and crumbled"
  - item: "capsicum"
    quantity: "1 medium"
    note: "finely diced; green"
  - item: "ghee"
    quantity: "2 tbsp"
  - item: "cumin seeds"
    quantity: "1 tsp"
  - item: "ginger"
    quantity: "1 tbsp"
    note: "finely grated"
  - item: "turmeric powder"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "hing (asafoetida)"
    quantity: "1/4 tsp"
  - item: "coriander leaves"
    quantity: "2 tbsp"
    note: "chopped"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1 tsp"

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a wide skillet over medium flame."
  - "Add the cumin seeds and allow them to crackle and brown."
  - "Add the grated ginger and hing; sauté for 20 seconds."
  - "Add the finely diced green bell pepper; sauté for 3 minutes until crisp-tender."
  - "Add the turmeric powder and salt; stir for 10 seconds."
  - "Crumble the fresh, warm, moist curd cheese directly into the skillet."
  - "Toss vigorously over medium-high heat for 2 minutes to coat the cheese in the spices without drying out its moisture."
  - "Remove immediately from the heat, garnish with fresh chopped cilantro, and serve warm."
---

Scrambled Curd Bhurji is a rapid breakfast preparation in Vaishnava temple communities. Using soft, warm curd cheese straight from the cheesecloth provides a creamy, delicate texture that contrasts with the crisp crunch of diced bell pepper.

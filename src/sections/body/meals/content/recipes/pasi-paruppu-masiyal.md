steps:
  - "Wash split yellow moong dal thoroughly."
  - "Pressure cook dal with 3 cups water, turmeric, and slit green chilies for 3 whistles until soft."
  - "Add salt and churn hot dal vigorously with a traditional wooden mathani until creamy and emulsified."
  - "Heat ghee in a small pan; fry cumin seeds until golden."
  - "Add grated ginger, curry leaves, and hing; sauté for 20 seconds."
  - "Stir sizzling ghee tempering directly into mashed dal."
  - "Remove pot from heat and stir in fresh lemon juice."
  - "Cover and rest for 5 minutes before serving with steamed rice and ghee."
---

Masiyal ('churned mash') is the gentlest course served in Tamil Vaishnava temple banquets. Yellow mung dal is recognized across Ayurvedic literature as the most digestible pulse, kindling appetite without producing metabolic heaviness.

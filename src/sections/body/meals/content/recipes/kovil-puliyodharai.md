  - item: "salt"

steps:
  - "Cook raw rice with 4 cups of water until firm; spread onto a wide platter to cool completely."
  - "Simmer tamarind extract with turmeric, salt, and 1 tbsp sesame oil over medium-low heat until oil droplets separate."
  - "Heat remaining 2 tbsp sesame oil; splutter mustard seeds, then fry chana dal, urad dal, peanuts, dried chilies, curry leaves, and hing until golden-brown."
  - "Stir puliyodharai spice powder and tamarind reduction into the hot seasoned oil."
  - "Pour hot mixture over cooled rice and fold gently from the bottom upward until every grain is coated."
  - "Cover and rest for at least 30 minutes before serving."
---

In the Sri Vaishnava temple kitchens of Srirangam and Tirupati, Kovil Puliyodharai is revered as the foremost travelling prasadam. The high concentration of wood-pressed sesame oil and concentrated tamarind acts as a natural preservative, keeping it fresh for days.

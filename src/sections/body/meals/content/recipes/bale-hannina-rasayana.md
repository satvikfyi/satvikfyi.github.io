Rasayana ('nectar' or 'rejuvenative elixir') is a traditional offering prepared during religious pujas and festival feasts at the Udupi Sri Krishna Matha. Combining ripe bananas (bale hannu) with raw coconut meat, fresh coconut milk, cardamom, and pure cow's ghee, it serves as an instant energy offering honoring the abundant tropical fruits of coastal Karnataka.

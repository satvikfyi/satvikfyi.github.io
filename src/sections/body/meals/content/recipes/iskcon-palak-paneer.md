  - item: "paneer"

  - item: "spinach"

    note: "stemmed, washed, steamed 3 minutes, pureed smooth"
  - item: "cream (heavy)"

  - item: "ghee"

  - item: "cumin seeds"

  - item: "ginger"

    quantity: 1

  - item: "hing (asafoetida)"

    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "garam masala"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1 tsp"

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a wide skillet over medium flame."
  - "Add the cumin seeds and allow them to crackle and brown."
  - "Add the minced ginger, minced green chili, and hing; sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Add the turmeric powder and the pureed spinach; simmer uncovered for 4 minutes."
  - "Add the pan-fried paneer cubes, salt, and garam masala."
  - "Simmer gently over low heat for 3 minutes to allow the paneer to absorb the spinach broth."
  - "Stir in the heavy cream and immediately remove the skillet from the heat."
  - "Cover and rest for 3 minutes before serving alongside warm chapatis or basmati rice."
---

In traditional ISKCON deity kitchens, Palak Paneer is prepared without onions, garlic, or excessive heavy spicing. Sweet dairy cream and caramelized ghee buffer the astringency of leafy greens, while ginger and cumin provide digestive warmth.

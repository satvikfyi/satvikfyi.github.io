steps:
  - Whisk the besan with water, turmeric, ajwain, ginger, chili and salt into a batter slightly thicker than for cheela.
  - Fold in the grated carrot, capsicum and tomato so every ladleful carries vegetables.
  - Cook on a ghee-smashed pan like the cheela, medium heat, edges lifting, one flip, but keep pudla slightly thicker and softer.
  - Stack on a plate under a cloth and serve warm with chutney.

Where the cheela is neat and round, the pudla is homely: vegetables scattered through the
batter, edges left lacy, ajwain humming underneath. Gujarati kitchens make pudla when the
vegetable box offers odds and ends, the recipe is less a formula than an allowance.

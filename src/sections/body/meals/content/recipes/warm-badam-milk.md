steps:

  - Simmer the milk in a small pot until it rises once, then lower the heat.
  - Whisk in the almond paste and simmer 4–5 minutes, stirring, until slightly thickened.
  - Crush the saffron between your fingers into the pot; add cardamom and sweeten with jaggery.
  - Strain into cups and drink warm, ideally an hour before sleep.

Milk is the Bhagavad Gita's own example of satvik food, and the tradition built a small ritual
around drinking it correctly: warm, spiced, unhurried, and not mixed with a meal. Badam milk is the
beloved version, almonds for substance, saffron and cardamom for fragrance, a little jaggery for
the sweet taste. Taken before bed it is the classic vāta-settler after a long, windy day.

ingredients:
  - item: daliya (broken wheat)
    quantity: ½ cup
  - item: water
    quantity: 2 cups
  - item: milk
    quantity: 1 cup
  - item: ghee
    quantity: 1 tsp
  - item: cardamom powder
    quantity: ¼ tsp
  - item: jaggery
    quantity: 1 tbsp
    note: or to taste
  - item: almonds
    quantity: 6
    note: slivered, soaked if possible
steps:
  - Warm the ghee in a heavy pot and roast the daliya over medium heat until it smells nutty, 3–4 minutes.
  - Pour in the water carefully, add a pinch of salt, and bring to a boil.
  - Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 12–15 minutes until the wheat is soft and the water is absorbed.
  - Add the milk and simmer 3–4 more minutes, stirring, until creamy.
  - Stir in cardamom and jaggery; top with almonds and serve warm.

Wheat is one of the most praised grains in the classical materia medica: sweet, heavy and building,
ideal when vāta runs high in cold weather. Roasting in ghee before boiling is a stock ayurvedic
technique, it makes grain lighter on the stomach and less gluey. The same base works savoury, with
cumin and vegetables, for a lunch version.

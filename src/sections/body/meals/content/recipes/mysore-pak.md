ingredients:
  - item: besan (gram flour)

  - item: ghee
    quantity: 1½ cups
    note: melted, warm
  - item: sugar
    quantity: 2¼ cups
  - item: water
    quantity: 1 cup
    note: for the syrup
  - item: cardamom powder
    quantity: ¼ tsp
    note: optional
steps:
  - Sift the besan; keep the ghee gently warm so it stays liquid throughout.
  - Boil the sugar and water to a one-string syrup, a drop between thumb and finger should form a short thread.
  - Lower the heat; add the besan in a steady rain, whisking hard against lumps.
  - Pour in the warm ghee a ladle at a time, stirring constantly; the mass will froth, seize, then turn porous and aromatic.
  - When it leaves the pan's sides and pulls from the ladle in ribbons, add cardamom and pour into a greased tray.
  - Mark into squares while warm; let it set fully before lifting the pieces out.

Legend gives Mysore pak to the palace kitchen of Krishna Raja Wadiyar, a cook who
poured ghee into a besan halva by mistake and served the result as a new sweet. Whatever
the truth, the recipe's character is royal: enormous quantities of ghee, constant
stirring, and no forgiveness for a cold pan.

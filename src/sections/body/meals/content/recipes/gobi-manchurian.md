  - item: spring onion
    quantity: 2 tbsp
    note: chopped, to finish
steps:
  - Make a thick batter of maida, corn flour, chili powder, pepper, salt and water; coat the florets well.
  - Deep-fry at medium heat until the florets are golden and just tender, 4–5 minutes; drain.
  - In a wide pan, heat a tablespoon of oil and flash-fry the garlic, ginger and chili until fragrant, 30 seconds.
  - Add the sauces and 3 tablespoons of water; boil to a sticky glaze.
  - Toss the fried cauliflower through the glaze, finish with spring onion, and eat immediately.

steps:
  - Whisk the besan with the water, turmeric, cumin and salt into a smooth batter the consistency of pouring cream; rest 10 minutes.
  - Stir in the coriander. The batter should coat a spoon; thin with water if it has thickened.
  - Heat a flat pan on medium and smear with ghee; pour a ladle of batter and spread it into a thin round.
  - Cook until the edges lift and the underside is golden, about 2 minutes; flip and cook 1 minute more with a few drops of ghee around the edge.
  - Serve hot with mint chutney or a spoon of curd.

"Sheela" is the family word for what most of India calls cheela or chilla, a savoury
chickpea-flour pancake that needs no fermentation, no grinding and barely five minutes of
rest. A later notebook page tucks a paneer, capsicum and corn stuffing inside the same
pancake, turning a light breakfast into a small meal.

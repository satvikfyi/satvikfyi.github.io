steps:
  - "Coarsely grind black peppercorns, cumin seeds, and dry red chili using a mortar and pestle."
  - "In a pot, combine thin tamarind extract, 2 cups water, turmeric, salt, and half the crushed spice mix."
  - "Boil over medium flame for 6 minutes until raw acid aroma mellows."
  - "Add decanted toor dal water; heat until broth begins to rise into an aromatic froth, then turn off heat immediately."
  - "Heat ghee; temper mustard seeds, remaining crushed spices, curry leaves, and hing."
  - "Splash sizzling tempering directly into rasam; garnish with cilantro and seal pot for 5 minutes."

Milagu Rasam (black pepper broth) is an Ayurvedic foundation in temple kitchens. Valued for stoking the digestive fire (deepana) and warming the channels, it relies on piperine from coarsely crushed black pepper rather than green chilies.

dietNotes: Reduce the milk well, the thickness of the base decides the creaminess, since there is no churner to do the work. Mango is considered heating in ayurveda, but ice-cold food balances that in small summer portions.
ingredients:
  - item: milk
    quantity: 3 cups
    note: whole milk
  - item: mango
    quantity: 2 cups
    note: ripe pulp, pureed (alphonso or similar)
  - item: sugar
    quantity: ½ cup
    note: or to taste, depending on the mango
  - item: cardamom powder
    quantity: ¼ tsp
    note: optional
  - item: mango essence
    quantity: 2 drops
    note: optional, per the notebook
steps:
  - Simmer the milk in a heavy pan, stirring often, until it reduces by half and thickens; cool completely.
  - Stir in the mango pulp, sugar, cardamom and the optional essence until the sugar dissolves.
  - Pour into a shallow metal container and freeze; every 30–45 minutes, beat the setting mixture hard with a whisk or fork to break the ice crystals; four or five rounds over 3–4 hours.
  - Serve in scoop-shaped balls, ideally the day it is made.

---

Before ice cream machines reached home kitchens, this was the method: reduce the milk
until it is nearly cream, then freeze it with interruptions, whisking each time to keep
the crystals fine. The mango does most of the work; the essence, as the notebook
records, is a two-drop courtesy.

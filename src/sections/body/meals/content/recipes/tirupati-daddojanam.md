  - item: "curd (yogurt)"

  - item: "milk"
    quantity: "1/2 cup"
    note: "boiled and cooled"
  - item: "ginger"
    quantity: "1.5 tbsp"
    note: "finely minced"
  - item: "black peppercorns"
    quantity: "1 tbsp"
    note: "coarsely bruised"
  - item: "mustard seeds"
    quantity: "1 tsp"

    quantity: "1 sprig"
  - item: "ghee"
    quantity: "1 tbsp"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1.5 tsp"

steps:
  - "Cook rice with 3.5 cups water until soft; mash lightly with the back of a ladle while warm."
  - "Combine mashed rice with boiled cooled milk, fresh whisked curd, and salt."
  - "Heat ghee in a small pan over medium flame."
  - "Add mustard seeds; when they crackle, add bruised peppercorns, minced ginger, and curry leaves."
  - "Pour hot tempering immediately into the curd rice and fold well."
  - "Rest for 20 minutes before offering or serving."
---

Unlike domestic curd rice, temple Daddojanam derives its distinct warmth entirely from the synergy between bruised black peppercorns and minced ginger, omitting green chilies in the orthodox Tirupati tradition.

steps:
  - Drain the soaked rice. Heat the ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot and sizzle the whole spices for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  - Add the ginger and vegetables; sauté 3–4 minutes over medium heat.
  - Fold in the rice gently, coating every grain with the spiced ghee for a minute.
  - Pour in the water with the salt, bring to a boil, then cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 12–14 minutes.
  - Switch off the heat and let it rest, covered, 5 minutes; fork through the garam masala and serve.

Pulao is celebration food in the satvik tradition, temple prasād kitchens across India make exactly
this kind of dish: rice, seasonal vegetables and whole spices, cooked in ghee without onion or
garlic. The whole spices are intended to be aromatics, not heat; fishing out a clove or cinnamon
bark on the plate is part of the experience, and a sign the cook kept the dish gentle.

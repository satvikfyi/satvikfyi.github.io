Panakam is the sacred rehydrating drink distributed at South Indian temples during Sri Rama Navami at the height of the summer heat. Ancient Ayurvedic texts classify Panakam as a prime electrolyte restorer: raw unrefined jaggery replenishes minerals and glycogen, dry ginger (sukku) aids hydration absorption, black pepper balances sweet heavy sugars, and edible camphor (pacha karpooram) imparts a cooling, spiritual aroma.

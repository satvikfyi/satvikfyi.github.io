  - Season with salt and simmer 3 minutes; finish with coriander and serve warm.

Ash gourd has the odd distinction of appearing in the classical "best of" lists, Charaka names it
among the finest vegetables, while being nearly flavorless on its own. That neutrality is the
point: it takes on cumin and curry leaves like a blank page, and its coolness is welcome when pitta
flares. In the yoga tradition the gourd even carries ritual weight; in the kitchen it simply
soothes.

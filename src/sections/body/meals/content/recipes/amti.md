steps:
  - Pressure-cook the dal with the water and turmeric until soft (4–5 whistles), then whisk it smooth and loose.
  - Heat the ghee, pop the mustard seeds, and add cumin, curry leaves and hing.
  - Pour in the dal, then add the goda masala, chili powder, jaggery, tamarind and salt.
  - Simmer 5–7 minutes so the sweet, sour and spice marry; loosen with hot water to a pourable dal.
  - Finish with coriander and serve with steamed rice or phulka.

Amti is the taste of a Maharashtrian thali: one dal carrying all the key flavours at once,
 sweet from jaggery, sour from tamarind or kokum, warm from goda masala. The name itself
is said to come from "amatya", sour; a reminder that this dal was being poured over rice
in these kitchens long before "dal tadka" was a menu phrase.

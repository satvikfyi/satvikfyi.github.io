steps:
  - "In a small saucepan, bring the whole milk and crushed saffron threads to a rolling boil; keep hot."
  - "Melt the ghee in a heavy brass or cast-iron karai over medium-low flame."
  - "Add the coarse semolina (sooji); roast slowly, stirring continuously with a flat wooden spatula for 12 to 15 minutes."
  - "Once the semolina turns aromatic, pale reddish-brown, and ghee begins to froth at the edges, lower the flame to minimum."
  - "Slowly pour the boiling saffron milk into the roasted semolina (pour with care, as steam rises rapidly)."
  - "Stir briskly to eliminate any lumps; cook on low heat for 3 to 4 minutes until the semolina absorbs all the milk and swells fully."
  - "Add the raw sugar and continue stirring."
  - "The mixture will liquefy as the sugar melts; cook over low heat for another 4 to 5 minutes until it thickens and glossy ghee beads separate from the pan walls."
  - "Fold in the cardamom powder, slivered almonds, and slivered pistachios."
  - "Turn off the heat and rest covered for 5 minutes before offering warm."
---

At the Shrinathji Haveli in Nathdwara, Rajasthan, Sheera (or Halwa) is offered during the early morning Mangala and Shringar darshans to warm the deity during chilly Mewari dawns. Following the golden rule of royal halvais (1:1:1:3 ratio of semolina, ghee, sugar, and milk), it forms an unctuous, restoring confection.

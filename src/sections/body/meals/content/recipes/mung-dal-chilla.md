steps:
  - Drain the soaked dal and blend with ginger, chili, cumin and turmeric, adding water a little at a time to a thick pouring batter.
  - Stir in the chopped coriander and salt; the batter should coat a spoon.
  - Heat a flat pan on medium, smear with a little ghee, and spread a ladle of batter into a small round pancake.
  - Cook until the edges lift, about 2 minutes; flip, add a few drops of ghee around the edge, and cook 1–2 minutes more.
  - Serve warm with mint-coconut chutney or a squeeze of lemon.

Chilla (or pudla) is the satvik cook's answer to a savoury breakfast pancake: no fermentation
waiting time, no flour, just lentils, spice and a hot pan. Mung holds a special place in the
classics, Vāgbhaṭa names it the best of the pulses; because it nourishes without heaviness, which
makes these pancakes a favourite spring food when kapha runs high.

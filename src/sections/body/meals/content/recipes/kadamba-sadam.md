steps:
  - "Pressure cook rice, toor dal, vegetables, 4 cups water, and turmeric for 3 whistles until tender."
  - "Dry-roast coriander, fenugreek, and chilies; grind with coconut and 3 tbsp water into a smooth paste."
  - "Boil tamarind liquid with salt for 5 minutes."
  - "Add cooked rice-dal-vegetable mash and spice paste to tamarind broth; simmer 6 to 8 minutes."
  - "Heat sesame oil; crackle mustard seeds and hing; pour over rice and stir."
  - "Rest covered for 10 minutes before serving."

Kadambam means 'mixture' or 'garland'. At Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple during the Margazhi festival, Kadamba Sadam unites seasonal indigenous produce and freshly ground spices into a single sacred offering.

ingredients:
  - item: moong dal (split yellow)
    quantity: ¾ cup
    note: rinsed
  - item: water
    quantity: 3 cups
  - item: turmeric powder
    quantity: ¼ tsp
  - item: ghee
    quantity: 1 tbsp
  - item: cumin seeds
    quantity: 1 tsp
  - item: 'hing (asafoetida)'
    quantity: 1 pinch
  - item: 'dry red chili'
    quantity: 1
    note: whole dried
  - item: red chili powder
    quantity: ⅛ tsp
    note: optional
  - item: rock salt
    quantity: to taste
  - item: lemon
    quantity: 1 tsp
    note: juice
  - item: coriander leaves
    quantity: 2 tbsp
    note: chopped
steps:
  - Pressure-cook the dal with the water and turmeric until very soft (3–4 whistles), or simmer covered 30 minutes.
  - Whisk the dal smooth; it should be pourable, add hot water if it has thickened.
  - For the tadka, heat the ghee until it shimmers, add cumin and let it darken a shade, then the dried chili and hing.
  - Pour the sizzling tadka over the dal; add salt and simmer 2 minutes.
  - Finish with lemon juice and coriander; serve with steamed rice or phulka.

Every Indian household keeps a version of this dal, the one you make when nothing else
is planned. The notebook records it in one line: moong dal, tadka of hing, jeera, ghee,
red chili. The tadka is added at the very end so its fragrance survives, and the whole
pot is soft enough to eat with rice and nothing else.

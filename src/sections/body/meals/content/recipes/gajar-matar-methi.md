steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a wide skillet over medium flame."
  - "Add the cumin seeds, minced ginger, and green chili; sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Add the chopped fresh fenugreek leaves; sauté uncovered for 3 minutes until completely wilted and dry."
  - "Add the diced carrots, green peas, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and salt."
  - "Toss well over medium heat for 2 minutes to coat the vegetables in the seasoned ghee."
  - "Cover the pan with a tight lid, reduce the flame to low, and cook for 10 to 12 minutes until the carrots are tender."
  - "Remove the lid and sauté over medium-high heat for 2 minutes to evaporate any remaining moisture."
  - "Serve hot accompanied by phulkas or dal and rice."

During the winter months in North India and Braj, tender red Delhi carrots, garden peas, and fresh fenugreek greens (methi) arrive simultaneously at the markets. Vaishnava deity kitchens celebrate this seasonal convergence in a harmonious three-part dry sabji: sweet, earthy, and pleasantly bitter (shad-rasa).

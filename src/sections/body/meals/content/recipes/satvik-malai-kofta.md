steps:
  - "In a bowl, knead together the grated paneer, mashed potatoes, cornstarch, and 1/2 teaspoon salt into a soft, smooth dough."
  - "Divide into 10 portions; flatten each, encase a golden raisin in the center, and roll into a seamless spherical ball."
  - "Heat the frying oil over medium flame; gently deep-fry the koftas until pale golden and crisp on the outside; drain carefully."
  - "Heat the ghee in a saucepan over medium-low flame; add the cumin seeds and grated ginger; sauté for 30 seconds."
  - "Whisk the cashew paste with the warm milk and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt; pour into the pan."
  - "Cook over low heat, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon for 6 minutes, until the sauce turns velvety and thickens."
  - "Stir in the green cardamom powder and heavy cream; remove from the flame immediately."
  - "Arrange the warm fried koftas in a wide serving dish."
  - "Ladle the hot white shahi gravy over the koftas immediately before offering."
---

Malai Kofta represents the peak of royal (shahi) vegetarian cuisine adapted to Vaishnava principles. Eliminating both onions and red tomatoes, this preparation relies on pure cashew milk, whole dairy cream, and green cardamom to produce an ivory-hued, delicate sauce.

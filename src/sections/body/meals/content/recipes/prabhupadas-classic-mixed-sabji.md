ingredients:
  - item: "eggplant"
    quantity: "1 medium"
    note: "cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - item: "potato"
    quantity: 2
    note: "medium, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes"
  - item: "pumpkin"
    quantity: "1 cup"
    note: "butternut or yellow pumpkin, cubed"
  - item: "ghee"
    quantity: "3 tbsp"
  - item: "panch phoron"
    quantity: "1 tsp"
    note: "equal parts cumin, fennel, fenugreek, black mustard, and kalonji"
  - item: "dry red chili"
    quantity: 2
    note: "broken in half"
  - item: "ginger"
    quantity: "1 tbsp"
    note: "finely grated"
  - item: "turmeric powder"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "water"
    quantity: "1/4 cup"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1 tsp"

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat."
  - "Add the panch phoron seeds and dried red chilies; fry until the seeds crackle and release their fragrance."
  - "Add the grated ginger, potato cubes, and cubed winter squash; sauté for 5 minutes until lightly browned."
  - "Add the cubed eggplant, turmeric powder, salt, and 1/4 cup of water."
  - "Gently turn the vegetables once with a large spoon to coat them in the seasoned ghee."
  - "Cover the pot tightly with a secure lid and lower the flame to minimum."
  - "Cook undisturbed for 20 minutes without stirring, letting the vegetables steam and caramelize in their own natural juices."
  - "Remove the lid; check that the potatoes are tender and the eggplant is melt-in-the-mouth soft."
  - "Gently fold once from the bottom to combine, taking care not to crush the vegetable chunks."
  - "Rest for 5 minutes before serving with hot pooris or chapatis."
---

This preparation is rooted in the Gaudiya Vaishnava lineage of Bengal. Taught directly by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada to early disciples, it demonstrates the power of simplicity: sweet winter squash, tender potatoes, and eggplant melt together under the aromatic influence of Bengali five-spice (panch phoron).

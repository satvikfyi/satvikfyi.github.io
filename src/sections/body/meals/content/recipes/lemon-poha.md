  - item: poha (flattened rice)
    quantity: 2 cups

  - item: peanuts
    quantity: 3 tbsp
  - item: ghee

    quantity: 8

    quantity: to taste
  - item: lemon
    quantity: 1 tbsp
    note: juice
  - item: coconut (fresh grated)
    quantity: 2 tbsp
    note: to garnish
steps:
  - Rinse the poha in a colander under running water for a few seconds, then leave it to soften and swell for 10 minutes.
  - Roast the peanuts in the ghee until they crackle; add mustard seeds and let them pop.
  - Add curry leaves, ginger, chili and turmeric; stir for 30 seconds.
  - Fold in the softened poha and salt gently, so the flakes do not break, and warm through for 2 minutes.
  - Take off the heat and stir in the lemon juice; garnish with coconut and serve immediately.

Poha shows up across Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh as a breakfast that neither sits heavy nor
leaves you hungry by ten. In ayurvedic terms flattened rice is considered lighter than cooked rice, 
useful when kapha is high, while lemon and ginger wake up the morning agni without heating pitta.

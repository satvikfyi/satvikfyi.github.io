  - item: "khoya (mawa)"

  - item: "almond flour"

  - item: "sugar"
    quantity: "1 cup"
  - item: "ghee"
    quantity: "4 tbsp"

  - item: "cardamom powder"
    quantity: "1 tsp"
  - item: "saffron threads"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
    note: "steeped in 2 tbsp warm milk"

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a heavy-bottomed non-stick or brass pan over medium-low flame."
  - "Add the crumbled khoya and blanched almond flour."
  - "Stir-fry over low heat for 8 to 10 minutes until the ingredients release a rich nutty aroma and turn a pale cream hue."
  - "Add the raw sugar and the saffron-infused milk to the pan."
  - "Stir continuously over medium-low flame as the sugar dissolves."
  - "Cook for another 8 to 10 minutes until all liquid evaporates and the mixture forms a soft, cohesive, fudge-like mass that pulls cleanly away from the base and sides of the pan."
  - "Stir in the freshly ground cardamom powder and remove from the flame."
  - "Turn the warm mixture out into a parchment-lined or ghee-greased shallow tray; press down firmly with the back of a flat bowl to smooth the top to a 1/2-inch thickness."
  - "Allow to cool completely at room temperature for 1 hour until set."
  - "Cut into elegant diamond-shaped squares and serve."
---

Sagar Bhog is a classic Dudhghar (milk-kitchen) creation of the Vallabh Sampradaya at Nathdwara. Devoid of wheat or grain starches, it relies entirely on reduced dairy solids (khoya) and tree nuts to create a confection offered during celebratory winter rajbhogs.

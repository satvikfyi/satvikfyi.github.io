ingredients:
  - item: basmati rice
    quantity: 1 cup
    note: or any everyday rice
  - item: water
    quantity: 1½ cups
  - item: rock salt
    quantity: to taste
  - item: ghee
    quantity: 1 tsp
    note: to serve
steps:
  - Rinse the rice in two or three changes of water until the water runs mostly clear.
  - Bring the water to a boil with the salt, add the rice, and stir once.
  - Cover and cook on the lowest heat 12–14 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  - Rest covered 5 minutes, fluff with a fork, and serve with a spoon of ghee melting over.

It may seem too plain to record, but the notebook's cook wrote it down anyway, and the
classical texts agree with the instinct: well-cooked rice with ghee is the baseline of
satvik eating, the food given to the unwell, the young and the fasting precisely
because it asks so little of the body.

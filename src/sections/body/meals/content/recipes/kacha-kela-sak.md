steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a wide skillet or wok over medium-low heat."
  - "Add the chickpea flour (besan), crushed mustard seeds, and crushed cumin seeds."
  - "Stir continuously for 3 to 4 minutes over low heat until the flour turns nutty, aromatic, and deepens in color."
  - "Add the steamed plantain cubes to the pan and toss for 2 minutes to coat with the roasted besan."
  - "Stir in the salt, jaggery, turmeric powder, and red chili powder."
  - "Add the steamed, chopped spinach to the pan."
  - "Gently toss all ingredients over medium heat for 3 minutes until completely warmed through and integrated."

Raw green plantains (kacha kela) feature prominently in Vaishnava temple cooking as an indigenous starch that predates the arrival of the New World potato. In this Gujarati-inspired preparation from Yamuna Devi, chickpea flour is roasted directly in ghee to create a savory crumb that binds the plantain and greens.

steps:
  - "Wash the rice thoroughly and drain in a colander for 15 minutes."
  - "Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat."
  - "Add 1 teaspoon of sugar and allow it to melt and caramelize to a deep amber syrup."
  - "Add green cardamom pods, cloves, and cassia bark; fry for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Add drained rice, cashews, and golden raisins; sauté for 3 minutes until grains are coated in caramelized ghee."
  - "Pour in boiling water, turmeric powder, salt, and raw sugar; stir once to combine."
  - "Bring to a rolling boil, then lower flame to minimum."
  - "Cover tightly with a lid and simmer for 15 to 18 minutes until all liquid is absorbed."
  - "Sprinkle freshly ground nutmeg powder over the rice."
  - "Gently fluff with a fork, cover, and rest for 10 minutes before offering."
---

Kanika is one of the most revered components of the Mahaprasad offered at the Jagannath Temple in Puri. The technique of caramelizing a small spoonful of sugar directly in hot cow's ghee gives the grains their characteristic amber hue and nutty undertone before the warming spices of the Rosaghara are introduced.

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a heavy-bottomed karai over medium-low heat."
  - "Add the coarse besan and whole wheat flour (atta); mix thoroughly to form a wet, sandy paste."
  - "Roast over low flame, stirring constantly with a flat spatula for 18 to 20 minutes."
  - "Continue roasting until the flours darken to a deep golden-brown, release an aromatic nutty scent, and the ghee begins to separate."
  - "Remove the pan from the flame and transfer the mixture to a wide, flat platter."
  - "Let cool for 15 to 20 minutes until the flour is comfortable to touch with bare hands but still warm."
  - "Sprinkle the cardamom powder, slivered pistachios, slivered almonds, and powdered bura sugar evenly over the warm flour."
  - "Knead the mixture gently with your fingers to integrate the sugar evenly."
  - "Take small portions of the warm mixture and compress firmly between your palms to shape into compact, round 1.5-inch laddus."
  - "Place the laddus on a tray to firm up completely for 30 minutes before serving."
---

Manohar Laddu ('heart-stealing laddu') is an ancient confectionery offering in the Pushtimarg temple network. Blending coarse Bengal gram flour with whole wheat atta supplies structural density, while confectionery bura sugar provides a delicate crunch against the rich cow's ghee.

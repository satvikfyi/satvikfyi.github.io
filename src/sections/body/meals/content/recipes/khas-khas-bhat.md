steps:
  - "Dry-toast white poppy seeds in a small skillet over low heat for 2 minutes without browning; remove immediately."
  - "Heat ghee in a heavy saucepan over medium heat."
  - "Add cumin seeds, cloves, and tej patta; fry for 45 seconds until aromatic."
  - "Add washed drained basmati rice and toasted poppy seeds."
  - "Sauté over medium heat for 2 minutes, coating each grain in ghee."
  - "Pour in boiling water, sugar, and salt; stir once."
  - "Bring to a vigorous rolling boil, then lower heat to minimum."
  - "Cover tightly with a lid and cook undisturbed for 18 minutes."
  - "Turn off heat and allow pot to steam covered for 5 minutes."
  - "Fluff gently with a fork and serve hot."
---

White poppy seeds (khus khus) have long held a place in North Indian and Bengali Vaishnava cooking. Toasted gently in ghee, the seeds release nutty pyrazines that cling to the long rice grains, providing a refined texture and balancing internal heat during warmer seasons.

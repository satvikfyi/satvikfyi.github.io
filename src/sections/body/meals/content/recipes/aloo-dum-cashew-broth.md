steps:
  - "Whisk the smooth cashew milk and fresh yogurt together in a small bowl; set aside."
  - "Heat the ghee in a heavy saucepan over medium flame."
  - "Add the cumin seeds and allow them to crackle."
  - "Add the grated ginger and hing; sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Add the turmeric powder and coriander powder; bloom in the ghee for 10 seconds."
  - "Lower the heat and pour in the cashew-yogurt mixture, stirring continuously with a whisk to prevent curdling."
  - "Cook over medium-low heat for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring frequently, until the sauce thickens and beads of ghee separate from the edges."
  - "Add the golden pan-fried baby potatoes, salt, and 1 cup of water; stir to coat."
  - "Cover tightly with a lid, reduce heat to the lowest setting, and simmer for 15 minutes (dum) to allow the sauce to penetrate the potatoes."
  - "Stir in the garam masala, turn off the heat, and rest covered for 5 minutes before serving."

In traditional Pushtimarg Haveli cooking, royal festive curries replace onion-garlic pastes with emulsions of soaked raw tree nuts and dairy. Pureed cashews supply plant lipids and soluble fibers, binding the sour yogurt into a cohesive sauce without commercial thickeners.

ingredients:
  - item: tomato
    quantity: 3
    note: chopped
  - item: garlic
    quantity: 3 cloves
    note: optional, sliced
  - item: 'dry red chili'
    quantity: 2
    note: whole dried, broken
  - item: oil
    quantity: 1 tbsp
  - item: mustard seeds
    quantity: ½ tsp
  - item: curry leaves
    quantity: 4
  - item: jaggery
    quantity: 1 tsp
    note: optional
  - item: rock salt
    quantity: to taste
steps:
  - Heat the oil and pop the mustard seeds; add the dried red chili, curry leaves and the garlic if using.
  - Add the tomatoes and salt; cook on medium, mashing, until they collapse into a thick glossy paste, 10–12 minutes.
  - Stir in the jaggery if using and cook a minute more.
  - Cool slightly; serve at room temperature with dosa, idli, paratha or phulka.

A cooked chutney keeps longer than a raw one, which is why every notebook has one: for
monsoon days when the raw mint chutney's freshness feels wrong, the slow-cooked tomato
version is exactly right.

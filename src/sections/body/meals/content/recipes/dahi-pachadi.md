steps:
  - Simmer the milk in a wide pan on medium-low for 20–25 minutes, lifting the malai (skin) that forms into a small bowl; reduce by about a third, then cool.

  - Fold in the fruits; chill 30 minutes.
  - Serve cold in small bowls, the pachadi should be eaten the day it is made.

Pachadi in this family's version is less the Kerala coconut-yogurt curry and more a
celebration of milk: boiled patiently for its cream, then cultured into curd and
sweetened with fruit. The black salt is the quiet masterstroke, a savoury shadow under
the sweetness that keeps it from cloying.

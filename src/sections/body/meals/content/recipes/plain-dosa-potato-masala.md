steps:
  - For the masala, warm the ghee and pop the mustard seeds; add cumin, chana dal, curry leaves, hing, turmeric, ginger and the optional chili.
  - Add the mashed potato with salt and 2 tablespoons of water; fold gently and cook 2–3 minutes. Finish with lemon juice and coriander.
  - Heat a flat tava on medium-high until a droplet of water dances. Pour a ladle of batter and spread it outward in a spiral, thin as you dare.
  - Drizzle ghee around the edges; when the dosa turns golden and lifts freely, place a spoonful of masala on one half.
  - Fold over, slide onto a plate, and serve immediately with coconut chutney.

Fermentation is the quiet satvik technology behind the dosa: rice and urad left overnight
until the batter breathes, then cooked to a shattering crisp. The potato masala tucked
inside follows the household's no-onion-no-garlic habit, hing and curry leaves do the
savoury work onions would do elsewhere.

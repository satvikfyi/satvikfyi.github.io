steps:
  - Heat the ghee and pop the cumin seeds; add ginger and the optional chili.
  - Add the tomato with turmeric and coriander powder; cook until soft, 3–4 minutes.
  - Add the peas with a splash of water, cover, and cook 3–4 minutes until tender.
  - Fold in the crumbled paneer and salt; warm through for 2 minutes, paneer needs no real cooking, just heat.
  - Finish with coriander leaves and serve with phulka or a small bowl of rice.

Fresh dairy in moderation is squarely satvik, the Bhagavad Gita's own list of good foods includes
milk, butter and cream. The key the tradition insists on is freshness: paneer made the same day (or
bought from a busy shop) is sweet and soft; older cheese turns heavy. Bhurji, a quick scramble, is
the kindest way to cook it, barely cooked at all.

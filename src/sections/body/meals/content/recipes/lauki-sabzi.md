steps:
  - Heat the ghee in a kadai and pop the cumin seeds; add asafoetida, turmeric and ginger.
  - Add the diced lauki and stir to coat for 2 minutes.
  - Add 2–3 tablespoons of water, cover, and cook on medium-low 8–10 minutes until the gourd is translucent and soft.
  - Stir in coriander powder and salt; uncover and cook off any excess water.
  - Finish with coriander leaves and serve with phulka or dal and rice.

Bottle gourd is unglamorous on purpose: over 90% water, easy to cook through, and (rare among
vegetables) classically described as cooling rather than heating. In the Indian summer, when pitta
runs high and appetites run low, lauki appears constantly: as sabzi, in dal, as a simple soup with
moong. Choose gourds that feel light for their size; heavy ones carry more water and less flavour.

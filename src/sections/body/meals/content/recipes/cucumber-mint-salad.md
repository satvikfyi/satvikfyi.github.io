In the Indian summer, when pitta blazes and even thinking about the stove is too much, this salad is
the whole recipe: cucumber for water and coolness, mint for its clean bite, coconut for a little
substance. Ayurveda's one caution about raw salads is timing, they sit best at midday, when agni is
strongest, and worst late at night.

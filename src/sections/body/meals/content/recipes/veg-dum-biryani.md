steps:
  - Boil the rice in 4 cups of water with the whole spices (tej patta, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, black cardamom, mace, pepper) and salt until 70% done; it should still bite. Drain.
  - In the same pot, heat 2 tablespoons of ghee; sizzle cumin, add ginger, turmeric, chili and coriander powders, then the vegetables; cook 5 minutes.
  - Lower the heat; stir in the whisked yogurt a spoon at a time so it does not split, with the cashews, raisins and salt. Cook until the vegetables are barely tender.
  - Layer the drained rice over the vegetables; dot with the last tablespoon of ghee, the saffron milk and coriander.
  - Seal the lid with dough or a tight foil and cloth; steam on the lowest heat (dum) 20 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before opening.
  - Open at the table, fragrant steam first, and fork the layers gently together.

Dum cooking, sealing a pot and letting it bake in its own steam; is Lucknow's gift to
the Indian kitchen. The notebook's version is gently spiced and onion-free, letting
mace, black cardamom and saffron do the talking. The seal matters more than the fire:
nothing should escape but the aroma when the lid is lifted.

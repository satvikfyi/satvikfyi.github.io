  - Finish with coriander and the optional coconut; eat warm or at room temperature.

The notebook opens with the humblest thing in it: a bowl of sprouts. Sprouting overnight
transforms stored legumes into living food, lighter to digest, richer in vitamin C; and
steaming keeps even chana sprouts kind to the stomach. It is the notebook's idea of a
default breakfast, the way an apple might be in another tradition.

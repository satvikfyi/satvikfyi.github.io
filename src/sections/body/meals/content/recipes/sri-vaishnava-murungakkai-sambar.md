ingredients:
  - item: "toor dal"
    quantity: "1 cup"
  - item: "drumsticks"
    quantity: 2
    note: "murungakkai, cut into 2-inch batons"
  - item: "tamarind"
    quantity: "1/4 cup"
    note: "thick extract dissolved in 1 cup warm water"
  - item: "sambar powder"
    quantity: "1.5 tbsp"
  - item: "turmeric powder"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"
  - item: "sesame oil"
    quantity: "2 tbsp"
    note: "wood-pressed gingelly oil"
  - item: "mustard seeds"
    quantity: "1 tsp"
  - item: "fenugreek seeds"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"

    quantity: 2
  - item: "hing (asafoetida)"
    quantity: "1/4 tsp"
  - item: "curry leaves"
    quantity: "1 sprig"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "2 tsp"
  - item: "water"
    quantity: "4 cups"

steps:
  - "Pressure cook toor dal with 3 cups water and 1/4 tsp turmeric for 4 whistles until completely mushy."
  - "Whisk cooked dal vigorously with a wooden churn until smooth."
  - "In a pot, combine drumstick batons, tamarind water, remaining turmeric, salt, and sambar powder."
  - "Boil uncovered over medium heat for 10 minutes until drumsticks are tender."
  - "Pour whisked toor dal into pot and simmer over low heat for 5 minutes."
  - "Heat sesame oil; bloom mustard seeds and fenugreek seeds until fenugreek turns reddish-gold."
  - "Add dry red chilies, curry leaves, and hing; pour immediately into bubbling sambar."
  - "Cover pot and rest for 10 minutes before serving with steamed rice."
---

Classical Sri Vaishnava temple sambar never includes onions or shallots. Instead, tender drumsticks (murungakkai), toasted fenugreek seeds, and compounded hing bloomed in wood-pressed gingelly oil deliver a rich aroma and savory mouthfeel.

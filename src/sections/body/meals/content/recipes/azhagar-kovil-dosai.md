  - item: "salt"

steps:
  - "Soak raw rice and whole black urad dal together for 4 hours."
  - "Grind coarsely with minimal water; ferment 4 hours."
  - "Fold in crushed peppercorns, cumin, dry ginger powder, hing, and salt."
  - "Heat cast-iron tawa; spread ladle of batter into a thick pancake (1/2-inch)."
  - "Drizzle 1 tbsp ghee around edges; cook on medium-low for 3 to 4 minutes until crisp and deep brown."
  - "Flip, add another tsp ghee, and fry until cooked through."
---

At Alagar Koyil near Madurai, these dosais are fried thick on cast iron with liberal cow's ghee. Whole black gram with skins lends rustic texture and hearty sustenance.

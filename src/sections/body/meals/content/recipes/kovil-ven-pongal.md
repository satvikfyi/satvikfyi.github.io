steps:
  - "Lightly dry-roast moong dal until aromatic."
  - "Combine rice, dal, water, and salt in a pressure cooker; cook for 4 whistles until creamy."
  - "Lightly mash cooked rice and dal with a wooden spoon."
  - "Heat ghee; fry cashews golden, then bloom cumin, crushed pepper, ginger, curry leaves, and hing."
  - "Pour sizzling ghee seasoning over pongal; fold gently and cover 5 minutes before serving."

Ven Pongal is the quintessential temple breakfast of South India. Ample ghee coats pulse starches, while black pepper and ginger stimulate the digestive fire (agni).

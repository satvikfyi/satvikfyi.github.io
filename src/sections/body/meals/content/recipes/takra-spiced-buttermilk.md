Few foods get the poetic billing takra receives in the Charaka Samhita: whole verses are spent on
spiced buttermilk's virtues, digesting meals and pacifying vāta and kapha alike. The trick is
dilution (takra is mostly water, churned cloudy) and freshness. Sour, thick, day-old yogurt
loses the sattva; whisked thin with cumin the same day, it becomes the tradition's answer to the
after-lunch slump.

steps:
  - Rinse and dry the herbs well, wet herbs make a watery chutney.
  - Grind everything with as little water as the blender needs, stopping to scrape down.
  - Taste and adjust, more lemon, more salt, or a pinch of jaggery to round it.
  - Keeps a day in the refrigerator under a thin film of water, but is best within hours.

Green chutney is the notebook's universal condiment, beside cheela, dosa, vada pav,
paratha. Mint is prized in the tradition as a cooler of both palate and pitta, which is
why the chutney turns up most often at the summer table.

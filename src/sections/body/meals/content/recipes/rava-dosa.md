    quantity: 1 tsp
  - item: black pepper

    quantity: 1 tsp

  - item: rock salt
    quantity: to taste
  - item: ghee
    quantity: 3 tbsp
    note: for the tava
steps:
  - Whisk the rava, rice flour, besan, curd, salt and water into a thin, watery batter; whisk in cumin, pepper, hing, curry leaves, chili and ginger.
  - Rest the batter 20 minutes, then whisk again and thin with water until it pours like milk; this batter must stay pourable.
  - Heat a flat tava on medium-high. Stir the batter, then drizzle it over the tava from a height in a spiral of drops; do not spread it; the gaps become the lace.
  - Drizzle ghee around the edges and over the holes; cook until the dosa is golden and lifts at the edges, 2–3 minutes.
  - Fold without turning, a rava dosa cooks on one side only, and serve with coconut chutney.

The rava dosa is a beautiful cheat: where a true dosa demands overnight fermentation,
this one stirs together in minutes and relies on a thin batter and a hot tava for its
lattice. The notebook records it in the fewest words of any recipe, hing, jeera; as if
the writer knew the batter by heart.

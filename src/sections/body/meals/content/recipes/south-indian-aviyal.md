steps:
  - "Place the baton-cut vegetables in a wide pan with 1/2 cup of water, turmeric powder, and salt."
  - "Cover and cook over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes until the vegetables are tender but still hold their shape."
  - "Place the fresh grated coconut, green chilies, and cumin seeds in a blender jar."
  - "Pulse with only 2 tablespoons of water to create a coarse, thick paste (do not puree smooth)."
  - "Add the coarse coconut mixture to the cooked vegetables; fold gently and simmer over low flame for 2 minutes."
  - "Remove the pan completely from the heat source."
  - "Gently fold in the whisked sour yogurt until evenly incorporated throughout the vegetables."
  - "Drizzle the raw virgin coconut oil directly over the top and scatter fresh curry leaves across the surface."
  - "Cover immediately with a lid to trap the raw coconut oil and curry leaf aromas, and rest for 10 minutes before serving."
---

Legend attributes the creation of Aviyal to Bhima during the Pandavas' exile. In traditional Sri Vaishnava temple feasts, Aviyal requires raw virgin coconut oil and fresh curry leaves folded in off the heat, preserving unheated coconut fats and volatile aromatic terpenes.

    quantity: 1

    quantity: "1/2 cup"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1/2 tsp"

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a saucepan over medium flame."
  - "Add the panch phoron seeds and dried red chili; fry until the seeds crackle and release their fragrance."
  - "Add the chopped tomatoes and salt; cook over medium heat for 5 minutes until soft and pulpy."
  - "Add the chopped soft dates, tamarind liquid, grated jaggery, and 1/2 cup of water."
  - "Simmer over medium-low flame for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the dates soften, integrate with the tomatoes, and form a glossy, thick jam."
  - "Sprinkle the freshly ground bhaja jeera saunf gunda over the surface."
  - "Remove from the heat, cover with a lid, and let cool to room temperature before serving."
---

Khatta (sweet-and-sour relish) is served toward the conclusion of the Jagannath Puri Mahaprasad feast. Simmering dried dates with country tomatoes, tamarind, and local jaggery produces a complex fruit relish that stokes digestive fire (agni) and clears the palate after grain and legume courses.

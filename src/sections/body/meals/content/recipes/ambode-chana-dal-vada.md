steps:
  - "Reserve 2 tablespoons of the soaked whole chana dal; place the remainder in a blender jar."
  - "Pulse the dal with the green chilies, grated ginger, and salt without adding water until coarsely ground, leaving several lentils whole."
  - "Transfer the coarse dal mixture to a mixing bowl; stir in the reserved whole chana dal, chopped fresh dill weed, and hing."
  - "Mix thoroughly with your fingers to form a thick, moldable mass."
  - "Heat the frying oil in a deep karai over medium heat."
  - "Take small portions of the dal mixture and flatten between your palms into flat 2-inch patties (about 1/3-inch thick)."
  - "Gently lower the patties into the hot oil in batches."
  - "Deep-fry over medium flame for 5 to 6 minutes, flipping halfway through, until brittle, crunchy, and deep reddish-brown."
  - "Remove with a slotted spoon, drain on paper towels, and serve warm."
---

Ambode is the celebratory crunchy fritter of Dvaita Madhwa banquets in Karnataka. Coarsely pulsing Bengal gram without water leaves whole lentils embedded in the crust, which roast to a nutty crisp during frying, while fresh dill weed (sabbakki soppu) infuses the fritter with aromatic herbal oils.

steps:
  - "Soak parboiled rice, raw rice, and urad dal together for 4 hours."
  - "Grind coarsely (rava texture) with minimal water; ferment for 8 hours."
  - "Fold crushed pepper, cumin, dry ginger powder, hing, salt, curry leaves, and warm ghee into batter."
  - "Pour into greased cups or leaf-lined molds, filling 3/4 full."
  - "Steam on high heat for 18 to 20 minutes until a skewer comes out clean."
  - "Rest 5 minutes before unmolding."

At Sri Varadaraja Perumal Temple, this festive idli is steamed in cylindrical bamboo molds lined with mandharai leaves. Heavy seasoning of black pepper, dry ginger, and ghee ensures long shelf life and easy digestion.

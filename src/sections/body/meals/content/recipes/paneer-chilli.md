ingredients:
  - item: paneer
    quantity: 250 g
    note: cut into batons
  - item: corn flour
    quantity: 3 tbsp
  - item: maida (refined flour)
    quantity: 2 tbsp
  - item: water
    quantity: 4 tbsp
    note: for the batter
  - item: capsicum
    quantity: 1
    note: sliced
  - item: onion
    quantity: 1
    note: quartered and separated
  - item: green chili
    quantity: 2
    note: slit
  - item: ginger (fresh)
    quantity: 1 tbsp
    note: julienned
  - item: garlic
    quantity: 4 cloves
    note: sliced
  - item: green chilli sauce
    quantity: 1 tbsp
  - item: soy sauce
    quantity: 1 tbsp
  - item: tomato ketchup
    quantity: 1 tbsp
    note: optional
  - item: rock salt
    quantity: to taste
  - item: oil
    quantity: 3 tbsp
    note: for frying and tossing
steps:
  - Whisk the corn flour, maida, salt and water into a thin batter; coat the paneer batons.
  - Shallow-fry the batons in hot oil until pale gold at the edges; drain on paper.
  - In a wide pan with a tablespoon of hot oil, flash-fry the ginger, garlic, green chili, onion and capsicum on high heat for 2 minutes; they should stay crisp.
  - Add the sauces with 2 tablespoons of water; let it bubble to a glaze.
  - Return the paneer and toss to coat for a minute; serve immediately, scattered with spring onion if at hand.

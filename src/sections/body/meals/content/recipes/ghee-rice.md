ingredients:
  - item: basmati rice
    quantity: 1 cup
    note: rinsed and soaked 20 minutes
  - item: water
    quantity: 1½ cups
  - item: ghee
    quantity: 2 tbsp
  - item: cumin seeds
    quantity: ½ tsp
  - item: 'green cardamom'
    quantity: 2
  - item: cloves
    quantity: 3
  - item: cinnamon stick
    quantity: 1 inch piece
  - item: 'tej patta (Indian bay leaf)'
    quantity: 1
  - item: rock salt
    quantity: to taste
  - item: coriander leaves
    quantity: 1 tbsp
    note: chopped, optional
steps:
  - Drain the rice. Heat the ghee in a heavy pot and sizzle the whole spices for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  - Add the rice and fold gently for a minute until the grains turn translucent at the edges.
  - Pour in the water with the salt, bring to a boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat 12 minutes.
  - Rest off the heat, covered, 5 minutes; fluff with a fork and scatter coriander over.
  - Serve with dal tadka, amti, or any curry that deserves better than plain rice.

Ghee rice is what the notebook makes when the dal is already good: rice cooked in ghee
and whole spices so the accompaniment becomes part of the meal. In the ayurvedic view
ghee is the ideal companion for rice, the fat "carries" the grain's sweetness and keeps
its heaviness kind.

This little dish has become ayurveda's calling card in the West thanks to Dr. Vasant Lad's cookbooks.
The reasoning is simple: raw fruit in the morning asks a sleepy agni to work hard, while stewing with
warming spices delivers the same sweetness pre-digested. A stewed apple is tridoshic-friendly when
spiced this way, the cloves help kapha, the ghee and softness help vāta, and the sweetness soothes
pitta.

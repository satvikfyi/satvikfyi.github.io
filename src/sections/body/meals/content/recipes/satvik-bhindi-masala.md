steps:
  - "Heat the mustard oil in a wide, flat skillet until it gently smokes, then lower the heat to medium."
  - "Add the cumin seeds, ajwain seeds, and hing; fry for 20 seconds until the seeds crackle."
  - "Add the dry cut okra pieces to the skillet in an even layer."
  - "Fry uncovered over medium-high heat for 8 minutes, turning gently every 2 minutes with a flat spatula without excessive stirring."
  - "Once the okra is tender, non-slimy, and lightly browned on the edges, reduce the flame to low."
  - "Sprinkle the turmeric powder, coriander powder, red chili powder, amchur powder, and salt evenly over the okra."
  - "Toss gently for 2 to 3 minutes until the ground spices form a toasted, aromatic crust on the okra batons."
  - "Remove from the heat and serve immediately with hot phulkas or parathas."
---

Cooking okra without onions requires two critical rules: the vegetable must be bone-dry before cutting, and it must never be covered with a lid while frying. Ajwain (carom seeds) provides thymol warmth that aids the digestion of okra's mucilaginous fiber, while amchur neutralizes any remaining starchiness.

steps:
  - "Place the drained fresh chhena on a flat work surface; knead with the flour and 1/2 teaspoon salt for 5 minutes until soft and pliable."
  - "Shape the kneaded chhena into flat 1.5-inch square patties."
  - "Heat 1.5 tablespoons of ghee in a skillet over medium flame; fry the chhena patties for 2 minutes per side until golden; remove and set aside."
  - "In the remaining ghee, brown the potato cubes for 4 minutes; remove and set aside with the patties."
  - "Add the remaining ghee to the pan; add cumin seeds and tej patta until aromatic."
  - "Stir in the ginger paste, turmeric powder, and cumin powder bloomed with 2 tablespoons of water; sauté for 1 minute."
  - "Add 2 cups of water, remaining salt, and the fried potatoes; bring to a boil and simmer covered for 10 minutes until potatoes are tender."
  - "Gently lower the fried chhena patties into the broth; simmer on low heat for 3 minutes."
  - "Sprinkle with garam masala, turn off the heat, and let rest covered for 5 minutes before serving."
---

In Bengal's Gaudiya Vaishnava monasteries, freshly split curd cheese (chhena) is preferred over pressed commercial paneer. The soft, porous patties soak up the ginger-scented cumin broth (jhol), yielding a tender, easily digested source of protein.

steps:
  - "Pressure cook toor dal and chopped tomatoes with 1.5 cups water and turmeric for 3 whistles until completely soft."
  - "Whisk cooked dal and tomatoes vigorously with a wooden churn into a fine liquid suspension."
  - "In a separate pot, combine 2 cups water, tamarind liquid, jaggery, salt, and Udupi Saarina Pudi."
  - "Bring to a boil over medium heat and simmer for 5 minutes to remove raw spice edge."
  - "Pour mashed dal broth into simmering tamarind liquid."
  - "Bring broth to a gentle rolling boil until a light aromatic froth rises to surface, then lower flame."
  - "Heat ghee in a small tempering pan; splutter mustard seeds and cumin seeds."
  - "Add curry leaves and hing; sizzle for 10 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Pour sizzling tempering directly over saaru."
  - "Stir in fresh chopped cilantro, remove from heat, and cover with lid for 5 minutes before serving."
---

At the Udupi Sri Krishna Matha, Saaru is served daily as the central broth over hot steamed rice. It balances sweet, sour, salty, pungent, and bitter notes using local tamarind, palm jaggery, and Saarina Pudi, embodying the Ayurvedic principle of shad-rasa.

steps:
  - Grind the coconut, tomato, ginger, one green chili and half the coriander powder with a little water into a coarse masala paste.
  - Heat the oil, pop the mustard seeds, and add curry leaves and hing.
  - Add the masala paste and cook 6–8 minutes until it darkens and the oil separates; stir in the chili powder, turmeric, goda masala and remaining coriander powder.
  - Add the matki sprouts with 1½ cups of water and salt; simmer covered 8–10 minutes until the sprouts are tender but intact.
  - Loosen with more hot water for a saucy usal; check the spice, misal should announce itself.
  - 'Serve in bowls: usal topped with a handful of farsan, a squeeze of lemon, and pav on the side to mop.'

Misal means "mixture": sprouts, curry and crunch layered in one bowl. Street versions
lean on onion and garlic, but temple-style and fasting versions prove the dish loses
nothing when hing and roasted coconut take their place. The notebook's masala, coconut
ground with spice, is the Kolhapuri heart of it.

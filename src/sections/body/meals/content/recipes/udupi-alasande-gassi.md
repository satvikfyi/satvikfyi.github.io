steps:
  - "In a dry skillet, roast coriander seeds, cumin seeds, and Byadgi red chilies over low heat until fragrant."
  - "Transfer to a blender; add the fresh grated coconut, tamarind liquid, jaggery, and 1/3 cup water."
  - "Grind into a smooth, thick, vibrant red masala paste."
  - "In a saucepan, combine the cooked black-eyed peas along with their cooking broth, the ground masala paste, and salt."
  - "Simmer over medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes until the curry reaches a rich, cohesive gravy consistency."
  - "Heat the coconut oil in a small tempering pan over medium flame."
  - "Add the mustard seeds and allow them to crackle."
  - "Add the curry leaves and hing; fry for 10 seconds."
  - "Pour the hot tempering directly into the simmering gassi."
  - "Stir gently, remove from heat, and cover for 5 minutes before serving."
---

In Udupi and across coastal Tulu Nadu, Gassi refers to thick, coconut-based curries that coat pulses or vegetables. Roasting the dry whole spices before blending with raw grated coconut allows the plant fats to emulsify into the bean broth, creating a stable, flavorful gravy.

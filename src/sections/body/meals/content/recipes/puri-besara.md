steps:
  - "In a heavy pot, combine the chunky mixed vegetables, 2 cups of water, turmeric powder, salt, and the dried mango slice (ambula)."
  - "Bring to a boil and cook covered over medium flame for 12 to 15 minutes until the vegetables are tender."
  - "Wet-grind the yellow mustard seeds and cumin seeds with 3 tablespoons of water into a completely smooth, velvety paste."
  - "Stir the ground mustard-cumin paste into the pot of cooked vegetables."
  - "Lower the flame and simmer gently uncovered for 3 to 4 minutes to marry the flavors."
  - "Heat the mustard oil in a small pan until smoking hot, then lower the heat slightly."
  - "Add the panch phoron seeds and dried red chilies; fry until the seeds crackle and pop."
  - "Pour the hot sizzling mustard oil tempering directly over the simmering curry."
  - "Cover the pot immediately with a tight-fitting lid to trap the pungent aromas, and rest for 5 minutes before serving."
---

Besara is an indispensable preparation of the Jagannath Puri temple kitchen (Rosaghara). Yellow mustard seeds (sorisa), ground cold with cumin seeds, create a pungent, emulsified sauce base that replaces onions. The dish is balanced with sun-dried raw green mango (ambula) and tempered exclusively in cold-pressed mustard oil.

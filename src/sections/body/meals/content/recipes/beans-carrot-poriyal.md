  - item: "carrot"
    quantity: "1.5 cups"

  - item: "coconut oil"

steps:
  - "Heat the coconut oil in a wide frying pan over medium heat."
  - "Add the mustard seeds, chana dal, and urad dal; fry until the seeds crackle and the lentils turn reddish-gold."
  - "Add the halved dried red chili, curry leaves, and hing; sauté for 15 seconds."
  - "Add the finely diced French beans and carrots to the pan."
  - "Add 3 tablespoons of water and the salt; stir to distribute evenly."
  - "Cover the pan with a tight lid and steam over medium-low heat for 6 to 7 minutes until the vegetables are crisp-tender."
  - "Remove the lid and sauté over medium-high heat for 1 minute to evaporate any remaining moisture."
  - "Stir in the freshly grated coconut, remove from the heat, and rest for 2 minutes before serving."
---

Finely dicing the vegetables ensures uniform heat transfer during a quick steam-fry. The pairing of sweet carrots and crisp green beans provides color and texture on the temple dining leaf, while the twin lentils (chana and urad) contribute a delicate nutty crunch.

  - item: "rice"

  - item: "coconut (fresh grated)"

    quantity: "1 tsp"
  - item: "urad dal"

    note: "halved"

    quantity: "1/4 tsp"
  - item: "curry leaves"
    quantity: "1 sprig"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1.5 tsp"

steps:
  - "Cook rice firm; spread onto a wide tray to cool."
  - "Heat coconut oil; crackle mustard seeds, then fry urad dal and chana dal until golden."
  - "Add chilies, curry leaves, and hing; sauté 20 seconds."
  - "Add grated coconut; stir-fry over low heat for 2 minutes without browning."
  - "Add salt, remove from heat, and gently fold into cooled rice."
  - "Rest 15 minutes before serving."
---

Thengai Sadam is a staple of Tirumala Annadanam halls. Gently warming fresh coconut in cold-pressed coconut oil preserves its sweet fats while infusing it with tempered lentils and hing.

steps:
  - "Pressure cook soaked black-eyed peas with 3 cups water and 1/2 tsp salt for 4 whistles until soft."
  - "In a small skillet, dry-roast coriander seeds, cumin seeds, and Byadgi red chilies over medium-low flame until fragrant."
  - "Grind roasted spices with fresh grated coconut and 1/4 cup water into a smooth paste."
  - "In a pot, combine cooked black-eyed peas along with broth, tamarind liquid, jaggery, and remaining salt."
  - "Stir in ground spiced coconut paste; simmer over medium-low heat for 8 minutes until aromatic."
  - "Heat coconut oil; crackle mustard seeds, curry leaves, and hing."
  - "Pour sizzling tempering directly over simmering saaru; let rest covered 10 minutes before serving."
---

Black-eyed peas (Alasande) thrive in the coastal climate of Karnataka. In temple community dining halls, this hearty pulse saaru provides concentrated plant protein, utilizing cold-pressed coconut oil and deep-red Byadgi chilies for rich color with gentle, warming heat.

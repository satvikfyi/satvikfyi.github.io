steps:
  - Warm the ghee and sweat the ginger for 30 seconds without browning.
  - Add the carrots and stir 2 minutes, then pour in the water.
  - Simmer covered 15–18 minutes until the carrots are completely soft.
  - Blend until silky (a hand blender in the pot is easiest), then return to low heat.
  - Season with salt and pepper; serve warm with coriander leaves on top.

This soup is sweetness managed by spice: carrot carries the sweet taste heavily, ginger keeps the
whole bowl from becoming heavy with it. In winter, when vāta's cold and kapha's damp arrive
together, a blended vegetable soup with ginger is the classical compromise; warm and moist, yet
clear enough not to sit on the chest overnight.

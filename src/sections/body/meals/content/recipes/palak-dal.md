    quantity: 1

steps:
  - Pressure-cook the dal with the water and turmeric until soft (4–5 whistles), or simmer covered 30–40 minutes.
  - Whisk the cooked dal smooth; add the spinach and tomato and simmer 5–6 minutes until the spinach is tender and deep green.
  - For the tempering, heat the ghee, pop the cumin, then add asafoetida and ginger; stir 30 seconds.
  - Pour the tempering into the dal with coriander powder and salt; simmer 2 minutes.
  - Finish with lemon juice and serve with rice or phulka.

Greens and dal together are a quiet staple rather than a showpiece, the kind of dish the classical
texts would approve of without ever naming it: leafy bitterness lighten kapha, the well-cooked dal
grounds the meal, and the small tempering of cumin and hing does the work garlic would do elsewhere,
without the rajas traditionally attributed to it.

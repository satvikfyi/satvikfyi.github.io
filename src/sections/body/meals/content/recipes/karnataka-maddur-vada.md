steps:
  - "In a wide mixing bowl, whisk together the fine semolina (rava), rice flour, maida, grated ginger, minced green chilies, chopped curry leaves, hing, and salt."
  - "Heat 2 tablespoons of peanut oil until smoking hot; pour directly over the dry flour mixture (it will sizzle vigorously)."
  - "Rub the scalded mixture between your fingertips until it resembles coarse, uniform breadcrumbs."
  - "Gradually sprinkle cold water, a tablespoon at a time, and bring the mixture together into a stiff, pliable dough (do not over-knead)."
  - "Divide the dough into 8 equal-sized balls."
  - "Place a dough ball on a sheet of greased parchment or banana leaf; flatten with your fingers into a 3-inch round disc of medium thickness."
  - "Heat 2 cups of oil in a deep karai over medium-low heat."
  - "Peel the flattened patty from the parchment and gently slip into the medium-hot oil."
  - "Deep-fry over steady medium-low flame for 5 to 6 minutes, turning occasionally, until the vada turns deep golden-brown, brittle, and crisp."
  - "Drain on paper towels and rest for 3 minutes before serving hot."
---

Originating in the temple town of Maddur along the Shimsha River, Maddur Vadas are renowned for their crisp outer crust and crumbly interior. Scalding the dry grains with smoking hot oil (moan) gelatinizes surface starches, ensuring an exceptionally brittle texture without leavening agents.

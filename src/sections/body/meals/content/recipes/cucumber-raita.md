  - item: black salt
    quantity: 1 pinch
    note: optional

    quantity: 1 tbsp
    note: chopped
steps:
  - Whisk the curd smooth and loose, thick enough to coat, thin enough to pour.
  - Fold in the cucumber (salt it lightly first and let it weep 5 minutes if it is very watery).
  - Stir in the roasted cumin, salts and coriander; serve chilled beside spiced dishes.

Raita is the Ayurvedic logic of opposites in a bowl: when the main dish is spiced and
heating, the side is cool and mild. Cucumber and fresh curd are both cooling; the cumin,
paradoxically, keeps the cold from dulling digestion.

steps:
  - Bring the water and milk to a gentle simmer with the pinch of rock salt.
  - Stir in the oats, lower the heat, and cook uncovered for 6–8 minutes, stirring often, until soft and creamy.
  - Mash the banana and fold it in while the pot is still on the heat, so its raw edge cooks off.
  - Take off the heat; stir in the cardamom, cinnamon and ghee, and sweeten lightly with jaggery if liked.
  - Serve warm, ayurveda favours breakfast warm and freshly made over cold or reheated.

Ayurveda treats breakfast not as the day's biggest meal but as a gentle kindling of the digestive
fire (*agni*), which is just waking at dawn. Warm, moist and lightly spiced grains are the classic
choice, precisely the qualities that settle vāta, the cold, dry, mobile dosha that accumulates
towards autumn and winter. Cardamom and cinnamon are traditionally held to kindle digestion without
the heat of chili or mustard.

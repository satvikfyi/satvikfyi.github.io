steps:
  - "Combine the washed yellow split peas, water, and turmeric powder in a large saucepan."
  - "Bring to a boil, skim off any foam, then lower the heat to medium-low."
  - "Cover partially and simmer for 25 minutes until the split peas begin to soften."
  - "Add the diced yellow pumpkin and salt to the pot."
  - "Continue simmering covered for another 15 minutes until both the peas and pumpkin dissolve into a thick, harmonious broth."
  - "In a small skillet, heat the ghee over medium heat."
  - "Add the black mustard seeds; when they crackle and pop, add the minced ginger and hing."
  - "Sauté for 20 seconds until fragrant, then pour the sizzling seasoning into the bubbling soup."
  - "Stir in the fresh lemon juice and remove from the heat."
  - "Cover and allow the flavors to infuse for 5 minutes before ladling into bowls."
---

Yellow split peas require gentle simmering to break down their firm starches. Combining them with winter pumpkin (kaddu) reflects classical Ayurvedic cuisine: the grounding sweetness of squash balances the astringent property of split peas, while cracked mustard seeds and ginger aid digestion.

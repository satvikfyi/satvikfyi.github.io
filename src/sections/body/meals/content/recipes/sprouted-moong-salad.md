Sprouting is the kitchen's oldest biochemistry: a day of water wakes the mung seed, softens it and
multiplies its vitamin C. Ayurveda appreciates the lightness (kapha's best friend) but counsels
vāta types toward a brief steam, raw and windy is exactly the combination the tradition warns
about. Either way, the lemon-ginger dressing is what makes the sprouts digest cheerfully rather
than sit.

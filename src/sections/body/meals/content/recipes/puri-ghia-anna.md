steps:
  - "Rinse the short-grain rice in cold water until the water runs clear; drain thoroughly."
  - "Heat cow's ghee in a heavy pot or earthenware vessel over medium flame."
  - "Add cumin seeds and tej patta; sizzle until cumin turns golden-brown and releases aroma."
  - "Add grated fresh ginger and drained rice to the pot."
  - "Sauté over medium heat for 2 minutes, coating every grain thoroughly in hot ghee."
  - "Pour in boiling salted water and bring pot to a rapid boil."
  - "Reduce flame to low and seal pot with a heavy, tight-fitting lid."
  - "Cook undisturbed for 15 minutes until moisture is completely absorbed."
  - "Turn off heat and leave pot covered for 10 minutes to allow steam to finish the grains."
  - "Gently separate grains using a flat spatula before serving."

In the ancient Rosaghara (temple kitchen) of Jagannath Puri, Ghia Anna (ghee rice) is cooked in porous earthenware pots stacked vertically above hearth fires. The fat-soluble aromatics of cumin and tej patta infuse the starch matrix, providing a grounding base for accompanying Dalma and Besara.

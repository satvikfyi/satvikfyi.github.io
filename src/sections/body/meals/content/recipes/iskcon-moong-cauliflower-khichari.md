steps:
  - "Wash basmati rice and split yellow moong dal together until water runs clear."
  - "Bring 6 cups of water to a boil in a heavy pot; add washed rice, dal, turmeric powder, and salt."
  - "Simmer uncovered over medium flame for 15 minutes, skimming any froth from surface."
  - "Add cauliflower florets and diced potatoes; stir and simmer for 10 minutes until vegetables are tender."
  - "In a small skillet, heat ghee over medium flame."
  - "Add cumin seeds and dried red chili; allow seeds to brown."
  - "Stir in grated ginger and hing; fry for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Pour hot sizzling ghee mixture directly into simmering khichari."
  - "Cover pot immediately with a lid and cook on low flame for 10 minutes until creamy and porridge-like."
  - "Stir in chopped cilantro, turn off heat, and rest 5 minutes before serving."

This preparation is the quintessential evening meal served across ISKCON temple communities worldwide. Split yellow mung beans break down into a smooth, digestible emulsion that carries the fat-soluble aromas of browned cumin seeds, ginger, and hing.

ingredients:
  - item: dates
    quantity: 1 cup
    note: soft variety, pitted
  - item: almonds
    quantity: ¾ cup
    note: soaked 4 hours and peeled
  - item: cardamom powder
    quantity: ¼ tsp
  - item: coconut (fresh grated)
    quantity: 2 tbsp
    note: or desiccated, to roll
steps:
  - Drain the soaked almonds and pat dry.
  - Pulse the almonds in a food processor to a coarse meal, then add the dates and cardamom.
  - Process until the mixture clumps together when pinched, a minute or two.
  - Roll teaspoonfuls into small rounds between your palms.
  - Roll each round in the coconut and set on a plate; they firm up nicely after 30 minutes in the fridge.

When the afternoon asks for something sweet, the tradition's answer is fruit, nuts and spice; never
refined sugar. Dates carry a dense, moist sweetness that is precisely what vāta craves in dry, cold
months, and almonds soaked and peeled lose the heat their skins carry. No cooking at all: the
sattva of the ingredients arrives unaltered.

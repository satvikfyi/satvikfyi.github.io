steps:
  - "In a mixing bowl, combine the squeezed grated bottle gourd, besan, ajwain seeds, and 1/2 teaspoon salt to form a thick dropping batter."
  - "Heat the frying oil in a wok over medium heat."
  - "Drop rounded tablespoonfuls of the batter into the hot oil; fry for 4 to 5 minutes until evenly golden-brown and crisp; drain."
  - "Heat the ghee in a heavy saucepan over medium flame; add cumin seeds and hing until sizzling."
  - "Add the pureed tomatoes, cashew paste, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and remaining 1 teaspoon salt."
  - "Cook over medium-low heat for 8 minutes, stirring continuously, until the gravy turns glossy and oil separates."
  - "Pour in 1.5 cups of water; bring to a rolling boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 5 minutes into a silky sauce."
  - "Carefully slide the fried lauki dumplings into the warm sauce."
  - "Simmer on low flame for 2 minutes to allow the dumplings to soak without breaking."
  - "Remove from the heat and rest covered for 5 minutes before serving."
---

Bottle gourd (lauki or ghiya) is prized in Ayurvedic cookery for its light, cooling qualities. In North Indian Vaishnava shrines, turning lauki into fried dumplings seasoned with digestive ajwain seeds transforms this humble squash into a celebratory course.

Standard Indian restaurant chutneys frequently rely on garlic and onions to supply savory body. In Vaishnava temple cooking, fresh garden mint (pudina) and coriander (dhaniya) are balanced through roasted cumin pyrazines, mineral rock salt, and fresh lemon juice, creating a clean, digestive herbal relish.

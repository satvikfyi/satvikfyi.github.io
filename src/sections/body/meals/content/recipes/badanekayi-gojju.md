steps:
  - "In a small dry skillet, roast coriander seeds, cumin seeds, white sesame seeds, fenugreek seeds, and Byadgi red chilies over low heat until the sesame pops and spices become aromatic."
  - "Allow the roasted spices to cool, then grind into a fine gojju spice powder."
  - "Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat; add the eggplant chunks, turmeric powder, and 1/2 teaspoon of salt."
  - "Sauté uncovered for 6 to 8 minutes until the eggplant chunks soften and brown on the edges."
  - "Add the tamarind liquid, powdered jaggery, remaining salt, and the freshly ground gojju powder."
  - "Simmer over medium-low heat for 8 minutes until the sauce reduces, thickens, and glazes the eggplant chunks."
  - "In a small tempering pan, heat a teaspoon of oil; crackle the mustard seeds, curry leaves, and hing."
  - "Pour the hot tempering over the gojju, stir gently, and let rest covered for 10 minutes before serving."
---

Gojju is a quintessential Karnataka temple preparation celebrated for its sweet-sour-spicy equilibrium. White sesame seeds (til) and fenugreek seeds (menthya) supply roasted pyrazines and earthy bitterness, balancing the sweetness of jaggery and the sourness of tamarind.

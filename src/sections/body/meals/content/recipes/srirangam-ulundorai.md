steps:
  - "Dry-roast urad dal, dried chilies, and black pepper on low heat until dal turns golden."
  - "Cool completely, then grind with hing into a semi-coarse powder."
  - "Heat sesame oil and ghee; splutter mustard seeds and curry leaves."
  - "Turn off heat; stir ground spice powder and salt into the warm oil."
  - "Add cooled cooked rice; fold gently until evenly coated, and rest 15 minutes."

Ulundorai is an ancient Tamil Vaishnava temple preparation offered on Saturdays at Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple. The roasted pulse powder lends an earthy, nutty depth without wet gravies.

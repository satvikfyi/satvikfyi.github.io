steps:
  - Warm the oil and pop the mustard seeds; add cumin, curry leaves, ginger, turmeric and coriander powder.
  - Add the sweet potato and beans; stir to coat for 2 minutes.
  - Pour in the water and half the coconut; cover and simmer 12–15 minutes until the sweet potato is tender but holds its shape.
  - Uncover and let the gravy reduce slightly; season with salt.
  - Finish with the remaining coconut and lemon juice; serve with rice.

Sweet potato arrived late to the classical ayurvedic table (the texts speak of ordinary potato and
yam), but modern ayurvedic cooks embraced it enthusiastically: it delivers the sweet taste the old
texts prize, the taste that builds and calms; in a vegetable that roasts, boils and simmers
willingly. Paired with green beans' astringency, the plate balances all six tastes more evenly than
most.

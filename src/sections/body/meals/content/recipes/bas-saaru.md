steps:
  - "Boil toor dal and chopped greens in 4 cups water until both are completely tender."
  - "Strain liquid through a colander into a bowl, reserving decanted broth for saaru."
  - "Dry-roast coriander seeds, cumin seeds, black peppercorns, and dried red chilies until fragrant."
  - "Grind roasted spices with fresh grated coconut, 2 tbsp of cooked dal-greens mixture, and 1/4 cup water into a smooth paste."
  - "Pour strained broth into a cooking pot; whisk in ground spice-coconut paste, tamarind liquid, and salt."
  - "Bring to a boil over medium heat and simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Heat ghee in a small pan; splutter mustard seeds and hing; pour hot tempering over bubbling broth."
  - "Cover and rest for 5 minutes before serving with hot rice."

Basida saaru literally translates to 'decanted broth'. In Karnataka matha kitchens, sustainability is inherent to food preparation: the nutrient-dense water drained from boiling fresh native greens and lentils forms this spiced broth, while the strained solids are tossed with coconut as a side course.

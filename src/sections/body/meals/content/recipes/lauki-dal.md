steps:
  - Pressure-cook the dal with the lauki, water and turmeric until soft (3–4 whistles), or simmer covered 30 minutes.
  - Whisk lightly, some pieces of lauki should remain for texture.
  - Heat the ghee, pop the cumin, add asafoetida and ginger, and pour the sizzling tempering into the dal.
  - Season with salt, simmer 3 minutes until the dal is pourable-creamy, adding hot water if too thick.
  - Finish with coriander leaves and serve warm with rice or on its own.

Lauki and moong is one of those pairings ayurvedic doctors prescribe rather than suggest, cooling
gourd, light dal, minimal spice. It is the archetypal "patient's dal" that turns out to be everyone's
comfort food: the gourd melts into the lentils, sweetening them quietly, and the whole pot cooks
faster than you would expect.

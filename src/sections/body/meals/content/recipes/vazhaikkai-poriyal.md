steps:
  - "Heat the virgin coconut oil in a skillet over medium flame."
  - "Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter."
  - "Add the urad dal, dry red chili, green chili, and curry leaves; sauté until the dal turns golden-brown."
  - "Add the hing and immediately add the drained, parboiled plantain cubes."
  - "Add the salt and toss over medium heat for 4 minutes until the plantain edges turn lightly golden."
  - "Add the freshly grated coconut and stir continuously over low heat for 1 minute to warm through without browning."
  - "Remove from the flame and serve warm as an accompaniment to rasam or sambar."
---

Vazhaikkai (green plantain) is an ancient staple in Tamil Nadu temple kitchens (madapalli). Cooked in cold-pressed coconut oil and seasoned with crunchy roasted urad dal and fresh coconut flakes, it offers grounding earthiness while honoring traditional South Indian temple dietary codes.

steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a wide, heavy-bottomed karai or skillet over medium heat."
  - "Add the cumin seeds and allow them to sizzle until golden-brown."
  - "Add the minced ginger, slit green chili, and hing; sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Add the potato cubes and cauliflower florets to the pan."
  - "Stir-fry vigorously over medium-high heat for 3 minutes, coating every vegetable facet with the seasoned ghee until the edges lightly color."
  - "Sprinkle the turmeric powder, coriander powder, and salt evenly over the vegetables."
  - "Reduce the heat to the lowest setting and seal the pan tightly with a heavy lid."
  - "Cook for 15 minutes, gently shaking the pan every few minutes to prevent sticking, until the potatoes and cauliflower are tender to the core."
  - "Remove the lid, sprinkle the amchur powder over the top, and raise the heat to medium-high."
  - "Stir-fry uncovered for 2 minutes to crisp the vegetable edges, garnish with fresh cilantro, and serve hot."
---

In traditional Vaishnava feast service, Aloo Gobi is prepared as a 'dry bhaji' rather than a watery curry. By allowing the salt to pull internal moisture from the brassica cells under a sealed lid, the cauliflower steams in its own concentrated aromatics without becoming waterlogged or mushy.

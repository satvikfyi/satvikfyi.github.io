steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a saucepan over medium flame."
  - "Add the cumin seeds; let them crackle and darken."
  - "Add the grated ginger and hing; sauté for 20 seconds until aromatic."
  - "Add the pureed tomatoes, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and red chili powder."
  - "Cook over medium heat for 8 minutes, stirring frequently, until the tomato paste thickens and ghee beads separate from the edges."
  - "Add the green peas, 1 cup of water, and the salt; bring to a boil."
  - "Reduce the flame and simmer for 6 minutes until the peas are tender."
  - "Add the fried paneer cubes and garam masala."
  - "Simmer gently on low heat for 4 minutes to allow the cheese to soften."
  - "Remove from the heat and allow to rest covered for 5 minutes before serving."

Matar Paneer is a staple offering across North Indian temples during festival feasts. By reducing fresh tomato pulp slowly in pure cow's ghee with freshly pounded coriander and cumin, temple cooks achieve a rich, clinging gravy without alliums.

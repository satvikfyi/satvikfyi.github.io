  - item: "white sesame seeds"
    quantity: "1 tbsp"
  - item: "chana dal"

  - item: "urad dal"

  - item: "dry red chili"

  - item: "hing (asafoetida)"

  - item: "curry leaves"
    quantity: "1 sprig"
  - item: "salt"
    quantity: "1.5 tsp"

steps:
  - "Dry-roast black and white sesame seeds until they pop; transfer to a plate."
  - "Dry-roast chana dal, urad dal, and red chilies until golden."
  - "Cool roasted ingredients, then grind with hing into a coarse powder."
  - "Heat sesame oil; crackle mustard seeds and sauté curry leaves."
  - "Remove from heat, mix spice powder and salt into oil, then fold into cooled rice."
  - "Rest 20 minutes before serving."

In Vaishnava shrines, Ellu Sadam is offered during the sacred month of Margazhi and Purattasi Saturdays to Lord Venkateswara. Sesame supplies warming energy and rich mineral depth.

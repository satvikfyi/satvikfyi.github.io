Marwari cuisine developed within an arid environment where fresh perishable souring agents were scarce. Vaishnava temple cooks solved this by utilizing sun-dried wild unripe mangoes ground into amchur. Blooming dry spices in water before hitting the hot ghee prevents the powders from scorching while releasing their deeper notes.

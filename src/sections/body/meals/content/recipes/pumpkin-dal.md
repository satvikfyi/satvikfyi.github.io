    quantity: 2 tbsp

steps:
  - Pressure-cook the dal with the water and turmeric until soft (3–4 whistles), or simmer covered 25 minutes.
  - Add the pumpkin cubes to the cooked dal with a little more water if needed; simmer 8–10 minutes until the pumpkin is tender and beginning to dissolve at the edges.
  - Heat the ghee, pop the cumin, add asafoetida and ginger; pour into the dal.
  - Stir in the jaggery and salt; simmer 2 minutes to marry the flavours.
  - Finish with coriander and serve warm with rice or phulka.

Autumn's pumpkin is the archetypal vāta-settler: sweet, moist and orange to its core. Cooked into
moong dal it makes a one-pot dinner where the pumpkin half-dissolves into a natural sweetness, the
hing and cumin doing quiet work so the sweetness never turns cloying. A spoon of ghee at the end is
not optional in the classical view, it "carries" the dal's goodness deeper.

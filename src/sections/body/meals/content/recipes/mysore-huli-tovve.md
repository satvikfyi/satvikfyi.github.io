steps:
  - "Pressure cook toor dal and diced ash gourd with 3 cups water and turmeric for 3 whistles until tender."
  - "Grind fresh grated coconut with 2 tbsp water into a smooth paste."
  - "Transfer cooked dal and ash gourd to a saucepan; add tamarind water, jaggery, salt, and sambar powder."
  - "Simmer over medium flame for 5 minutes to cook out raw tamarind edge."
  - "Stir ground coconut paste into dal; simmer gently for another 3 minutes."
  - "Heat ghee in a small pan over medium heat; add mustard seeds, curry leaves, and hing until popping."
  - "Pour hot ghee tempering into stew, stir gently, and keep covered for 5 minutes before serving."

While standard tovve is a plain boiled dal preparation, Huli Tovve blends sour tamarind (huli) and jaggery into the lentil base along with mild ash gourd (boodu kumbalakai), creating a versatile everyday dish across Mysore and Udupi Madhwa households.

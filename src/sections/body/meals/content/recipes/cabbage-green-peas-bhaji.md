steps:
  - "Heat the ghee in a wide frying pan or wok over medium flame."
  - "Add the fennel seeds, grated ginger, and hing; sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Add the shredded green cabbage and sweet green peas."
  - "Stir-fry vigorously over high heat for 3 minutes to coat the vegetables in the flavored fat."
  - "Sprinkle the turmeric powder and salt evenly over the vegetables."
  - "Reduce heat to low, cover the pan with a tight lid, and steam-cook for 5 to 6 minutes until the cabbage is crisp-tender."
  - "Uncover the pan, increase the flame to medium-high, and sauté for 2 minutes to evaporate any residual surface moisture."
  - "Remove from the heat and serve immediately while hot and crisp."

In Hare Krishna deity kitchens, shredded cabbage is cooked swiftly to prevent the development of sulfurous over-steamed odors. The sweet licorice notes of whole fennel seeds (saunf) and minced ginger lift the earthy brassica notes into a light, refreshing accompaniment.

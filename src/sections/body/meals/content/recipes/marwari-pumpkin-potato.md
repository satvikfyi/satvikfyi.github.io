steps:
  - "Heat the mustard oil in a heavy pan until it reaches smoking point, then reduce the heat to medium-low."
  - "Add the fennel seeds, fenugreek seeds, and hing; fry until the seeds are fragrant and fenugreek is reddish-gold."
  - "Add the diced potatoes and pumpkin cubes; toss over medium heat for 3 minutes."
  - "Add the turmeric powder, coriander powder, salt, and 2 tablespoons of water."
  - "Cover the pan tightly with a lid and cook on low heat for 12 minutes until the vegetables soften."
  - "Remove the lid and add the grated jaggery and amchur powder."
  - "Raise the heat to medium-high and toss gently for 3 minutes until the melting jaggery forms a glossy, clingy glaze around the vegetables."
  - "Remove from the heat and allow to rest for 5 minutes before serving."
---

Known as Khatta Meetha Kaddu, this dish is a cornerstone of Marwari Vaishnava feasts and temple offerings across Rajasthan. The bitterness of toasted fenugreek (methi) seeds cuts the natural sugars of yellow pumpkin, while sun-dried green mango (amchur) balances the jaggery.

    quantity: 2 cups

steps:
  - Drain the urad dal thoroughly; grind to a thick batter, adding water a teaspoon at a time; the batter should be stiff enough to hold shape.
  - Beat the batter with a spoon or whisk for 2–3 minutes until it turns pale, light and fluffy; a small ball should float in a bowl of water.
  - Fold in the ginger, green chili, curry leaves, pepper, hing, salt and the optional rice flour.
  - Wet your palm, shape a ball of batter, pierce a hole in the centre with your thumb, and slide it into medium-hot oil.
  - Fry, turning once, until deep golden and crisp, 4–5 minutes; the oil should bubble steadily, not roar.
  - Drain on paper and serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar, or soak in warm sambar rasam-style, as the household does.

"Medu" means soft in Kannada, and softness is the entire discipline of this vada: urad
dal ground thick, beaten full of air, fried patient and golden. Rainy-day food across
the south, vata-aggravating weather met with a warm, unctuous, grounding vada, which
is exactly how the ayurvedic kitchen thinks.

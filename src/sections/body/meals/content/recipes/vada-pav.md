steps:
  - 'For the filling: heat a teaspoon of oil, pop the mustard seeds, add curry leaves, hing, turmeric, ginger and green chili; fold into the mashed potato with lemon juice and salt.'

  - Shape into 8 rounds and chill while you make the batter.
  - Whisk the besan, water, chili powder and salt into a thick batter that coats the back of a spoon.
  - Dip each potato round in the batter and deep-fry at medium heat until golden, 3–4 minutes; drain.
  - Split the pav, smear green chutney and tamarind-date chutney inside, nestle a hot vada in, and press gently.

Vada pav is Mumbai in a bun, carbo-fuel for mill workers that became the city's
signature. The notebook records the whole assembly, including its two chutneys. A satvik
kitchen keeps the concept and drops the onion and garlic: the potato filling here leans
on curry leaves and hing, and nobody at the table has ever missed the rest.

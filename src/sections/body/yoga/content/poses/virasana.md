---
name: Hero Pose
slug: virasana
sanskritName: Vīrāsana
category: seated
level: beginner
summary: Kneel and sit between the heels, an upright, dignified seat that keeps the spine tall and the digestion comfortable after meals.
benefits:
  - Stretches the thighs, knees and ankles
  - Encourages an effortlessly upright spine for breathing and meditation
  - Traditionally recommended after eating to support digestion
  - Reduces swelling in the legs after long standing
contraindications:
  - Knee or ankle injuries, sit on a block or cushion between the heels, or avoid
  - Very tight thighs, place a folded blanket under the sitting bones
  - Recent knee surgery, seek a teacher's guidance before attempting
steps:
  - Kneel on the floor with the inner knees together and the feet slightly wider than the hips.
  - Ease the sitting bones down between the heels, toes pointing straight back.
  - Sit on a block or cushion placed between the heels if the knees complain at all.
  - Rest the hands on the thighs, lengthen the spine, and soften the shoulders down.
  - Breathe for 8–20 breaths, or as long as the knees stay quiet and comfortable.
breathingPattern: Free, easy nasal breathing; the lifted torso invites a full, unhurried breath.
associatedDoshas:
  - pitta
  - vata

Vīra means hero, the seat of one poised and awake. Alongside Vajrāsana it is the traditional
after-meal posture of ayurvedic household wisdom: upright, at ease, and kind to agni. Supported well
(with height under the hips), it suits almost every body.

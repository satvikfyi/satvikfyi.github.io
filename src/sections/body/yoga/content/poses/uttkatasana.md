---
name: Chair Pose
slug: uttkatasana
sanskritName: Utkaṭāsana
category: standing
level: beginner
summary: Sit back into an imaginary chair, a heating, strengthening squat that builds heat in the thighs and steady determination in the mind.
benefits:
  - Strengthens the thighs, ankles and calves
  - Stimulates the heart and circulation
  - Firms the abdomen and stretches the Achilles tendons
  - Builds heat and stamina, good for cold, sluggish days
contraindications:
  - Knee injuries, reduce the depth or practise near a wall
  - Low blood pressure, come up slowly at the end
  - Heavy menstruation or fever, prefer gentler standing poses
steps:
  - Stand in Tāḍāsana with the feet together or hip-width apart.
  - Inhale and raise the arms overhead, palms facing or shoulder-width apart.
  - Exhale and bend the knees, sitting the hips back as if lowering into a chair; knees stay over the toes.
  - Keep the weight in the heels, the spine long and the ribs drawn gently in.
  - Hold 3–8 breaths, then inhale to straighten the legs and exhale the arms down.
breathingPattern: Inhale to rise and lift the arms; exhale to sit down; breathe steadily while holding.
associatedDoshas:
  - kapha
sources:
  - title: Light on Yoga
    author: B. K. S. Iyengar
    reference: Utkaṭāsana
  - title: Asana Pranayama Mudra Bandha
    author: Swami Satyananda Saraswati
---

Utkaṭa means "powerful, fierce" in Sanskrit; the name itself signals this pose's nature. Its heat and
effort make it a classic friend of kapha constitution, waking up heavy mornings, though pitta types
should hold it for fewer breaths.

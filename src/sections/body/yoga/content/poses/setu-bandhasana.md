---
name: Bridge Pose
slug: setu-bandhasana
sanskritName: Setu Bandhāsana
category: backbend
level: beginner
summary: Lie back, feet planted, and lift the hips into a low, sturdy bridge; a safe, rejuvenating backbend that both calms and energises.
benefits:
  - Strengthens the back, glutes and hamstrings
  - Opens the chest and shoulders, easing desk posture
  - Stimulates the abdominal organs and thyroid in tradition
  - Calms anxiety while gently lifting energy, soothing for vāta nerves
contraindications:
  - Neck injuries, avoid turning the head while lifted; practise with a teacher's guidance
  - Late pregnancy, keep the lift small and supported
  - Lower-back pain, reduce the lift, or place a block under the sacrum for a supported bridge
steps:
  - Lie on the back, knees bent, feet hip-width and close to the sitting bones.
  - Let the arms rest beside the body, fingertips grazing the heels.
  - Exhale, press the feet down, and peel the hips up off the floor; thighs and knees stay parallel.
  - Interlace the hands under the back or hold the ankles; broaden the chest toward the chin.
  - Hold 5–8 breaths, then roll down slowly, one vertebra at a time.
breathingPattern: Exhale to lift, breathe evenly across the chest while holding, exhale to lower slowly.

A setu is a bridge, and this one carries traffic both ways: it lifts and brightens a kapha morning,
and settles a frazzled vāta evening. The supported version, a block under the sacrum; turns it into
almost pure restoration before sleep.

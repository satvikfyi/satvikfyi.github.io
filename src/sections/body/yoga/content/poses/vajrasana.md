---
name: Thunderbolt Pose
slug: vajrasana
sanskritName: Vajrāsana
category: seated
level: beginner
summary: The firm kneeling seat, vajra means diamond-thunderbolt; the one classical pose traditionally practised right after eating.
benefits:
  - Supports digestion when sat in after meals (traditional ayurvedic guidance)
  - Strengthens the pelvic floor and steadies the posture
  - Stretches the front of the ankles and the thighs
  - Calms and grounds, a stable seat for breathing practices
contraindications:
  - Knee or ankle injuries, sit raised on a block or cushion, or choose a chair
  - Pregnancy, sit with the knees wide and the torso supported
  - Circulation issues in the legs, change position if the feet go numb
steps:
  - Kneel and sit back on the heels, knees together or a little apart.
  - Rest the hands on the thighs, palms down; grow tall through the spine.
  - Let the shoulders soften and the chin sit level; breathe slowly.
  - Sit for 10–20 breaths, or a few minutes after meals, as tradition suggests.
breathingPattern: Slow and even; traditionally practised in silence right after eating.
associatedDoshas:
  - vata
  - pitta
  - kapha

  - title: Ayurvedic Cooking for Self-Healing

The thunderbolt seat is tridoshic by reputation: firm enough to settle vāta, cool enough to spare
pitta, and light enough on kapha to be the classical after-dinner seat. Ayurvedic households have sat
this way at the end of a meal for generations, a small habit with a long lineage.

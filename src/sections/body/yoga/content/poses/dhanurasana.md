---
name: Bow Pose
slug: dhanurasana
sanskritName: Dhanurāsana
category: backbend
level: intermediate
summary: Grab the ankles, lift, and rock gently; the whole body strung taut like an archer's bow, a strong heating backbend for the front line.
benefits:
  - Deeply stretches the chest, shoulders, abdomen, thighs and ankles
  - Strengthens the back muscles and improves posture
  - Strongly stimulates digestion and appetite
  - Invigorates and warms, a pose of energy and courage
contraindications:
  - Pregnancy, recent abdominal surgery or hernia; avoid
  - High blood pressure or heart conditions, prefer gentler backbends
  - Neck injury, keep the head in line with the chest, not thrown back
  - Lower-back pain during the pose, lower immediately and rest
steps:
  - Lie face down; bend the knees and reach back to hold the ankles (or a strap looped around them).
  - Exhale to firm the belly; press the pubic bone toward the floor.
  - Inhale and kick the shins back and up, letting the chest and thighs lift together.
  - Keep the knees hip-width and the shoulders melting away from the ears; breathe 3–5 breaths.
  - Exhale, release the ankles, and lower; rest the cheek on the mat before repeating once.
breathingPattern: Inhale to lift; the held pose allows only small, steady breaths; never hold the breath.
associatedDoshas:
  - kapha
sources:
  - title: Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā
    author: Svātmārāma
    reference: chapter 1, holding the toes with the hands, the bow among the chief āsanas
  - title: Light on Yoga
    author: B. K. S. Iyengar
    reference: Dhanurāsana
---

Dhanu is a bow, and in the Gītā's first chapter the whole world is one; the body strung, the breath
the arrow. As a strong, heating backbend it belongs to kapha: it burns heaviness and wakes dormant
strength. Vāta and pitta bodies can enjoy it briefly, warmed up and unforced.

indications:
  - The most digestible protein of the tradition, food even for the convalescent

  - Gentle on the kidneys and easy on weak digestion (laghu)

Every tradition keeps one food for the days when the body asks for nothing, and here it is mudga:
split mung, soft-cooked. The texts rank it first among pulses precisely because it nourishes without
burdening, the definition of satvik protein.

indications:
  - Traditional wound and skin herb (vraṇa-ropana), used internally and as a paste
  - Kindles digestion (dīpana) and is used for puffy, kapha-type swellings
  - Blood-purifier (rakta-śodhaka) of the classical lists
  - A staple of respiratory support in home remedy tradition
contraindications:
  - Gallstones or bile-duct obstruction
  - Can aggravate pitta, heartburn, heat rashes, ulcers in excess
  - High-dose extracts with anticoagulants or before surgery, consult a physician
  - Concentrated supplements in pregnancy, culinary amounts are traditional and fine
preparation: "In the kitchen: ¼–½ tsp cooked into dal, vegetables or rice; always with a little fat
  and, classically, a pinch of black pepper. As golden milk: ¼ tsp simmered in a cup of milk with
  pepper and a little jaggery. Externally: a paste with honey or water for small skin complaints, 
  patch-test first, as it can stain and irritate."

Haridrā means "the yellow one", and yellow is exactly what it does, to curries, to cloth, to
weddings, to inflammation in the classical telling. It is the everyday proof that in ayurveda the
spice rack and the medicine cabinet were always one cupboard.

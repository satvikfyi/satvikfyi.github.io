  - Premier medhya rasāyana, traditionally used for memory and clarity

Named for Brahmā, the creator, brāhmī sits at the top of the medhya (intellect-supporting) herbs of
the classical texts. Its coolness is the point: it is the classic tonic for pitta-bright, overheated
minds, students, worriers, and anyone whose thoughts run loud at midnight.

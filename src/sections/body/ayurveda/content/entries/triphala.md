Three fruits, five tastes, three doshas balanced: each fruit in triphalā leans toward one dosha, 
harītakī for vāta, āmalakī for pitta, bibhītakī for kapha; which is why the blend is the tradition's
default daily tonic. It works with the body's own rhythm rather than forcing it, which is the whole
ayurvedic idea of gentle.

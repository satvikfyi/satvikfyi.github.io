Śatāvarī, "a hundred roots", or by a old wordplay, "a hundred husbands"; is the tradition's great
nourishing tonic for the female body across its whole arc. Where aśvagandha warms and builds,
śatāvarī cools and fills; the two roots frame each other in the classical materia medica.

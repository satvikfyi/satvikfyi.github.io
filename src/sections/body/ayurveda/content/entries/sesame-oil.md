Taila, the word simply means "oil", because for the tradition this was the oil. Warming, fine enough
to slip into small channels (sūkṣma), it is the daily answer to vāta's dryness: a few quiet minutes
of warm-oil massage before the bath is one of the most beloved of all ayurvedic routines.

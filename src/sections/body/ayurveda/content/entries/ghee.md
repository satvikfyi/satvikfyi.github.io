Ghṛta is ayurveda's most quoted food-medicine: sweet, cooling and (the texts insist)
intelligence-carrying (medhya), which is why so many classical medicines are cooked into it. Modern
clarity: it is still butterfat, a saturated fat to enjoy as a condiment, not a course.

indications:
  - The everyday staple that balances all constitutions
  - Strengthening yet light (balya and laghu at once), suited to the weak and the well
  - Cooked extra-soft as peya (rice gruel), the classical food of fever and recovery
  - Its cooling sweetness makes it the classic pitta-season grain
contraindications:
  - Diabetes or insulin resistance, portion mindfully (white rice is quick to absorb)
  - Kapha excess, eat fluffy-cooked with warming spices rather than sticky-soft
preparation: Rinse and soak 30 minutes where possible. Cook with less water for separate,
  kapha-friendly grains; with more water and longer for the soft, vāta-friendly form. With ghee,
  cumin and rock salt it becomes the classical restorative.

Rice is the grain the classical dietetics keep returning to, praised as sweet, cooling and
strengthening. Aged basmati, stored a year like the texts advise, is lighter still; the smell of it
cooking is one of satvik living's small pleasures.
